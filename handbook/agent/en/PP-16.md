# PP-16 · Prism filter / Split-prism image

**Aliases:** Prism filter; Split-prism; Kaleidoscopic lens; Glass shard repeat; 棱鏡濾鏡; 分像效果; 萬花筒鏡頭; プリズムフィルター; 分身効果; 万華鏡フィルター

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Lens simulation

## What you see

Part of the picture repeats, flips, or fractures into glass-like copies while the main subject remains readable.

## Not to be confused with

Not warp/distortion (PP-09). Not chromatic fringing (REN-36). Not light ghosts/flares (REN-35). This duplicates recognizable scene content, not just light spots or RGB outlines. Keep separate from split diopters (REN-34), which concern simultaneous focus.

## Use it for

Hallucination, surreal cutaways, music visuals, fractured perception.

## Tunable parameters

shard count · angle · offset · opacity · edge mask (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Activate fractured perception → selected frame edges → introduce two or three angled/reflected copies that move coherently with the current image → retract those copies on exit.

## Implementation hint

Composite 2–3 masked, transformed copies of the colour buffer (rotate/flip/offset) along frame edges. Drive transforms from the live frame so motion matches. Keep centre subject mostly unobstructed.
