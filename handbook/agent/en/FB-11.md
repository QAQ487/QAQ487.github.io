# FB-11 · Freeze-frame finisher

**Aliases:** Time-stop finisher; Kill freeze; Final-hit freeze

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Time manipulation

## What you see

On the final blow the frame stops completely for ~0.3–1 s, often going monochrome/high-contrast with a darkened background and speed lines from the impact, then playback resumes with the defeat.

## Not to be confused with

Not regular hitstop (a few frames on every hit); this is longer, reserved for the decisive hit, and usually stylised. The film freeze frame is the same idea.

## Use it for

Boss final hits, end of a wave or round, super moves — giving weight to "this hit decided it".

## Tunable parameters

freeze length 0.3–1.0 s · style (mono / invert / colour key) · radial lines · camera push · resume easing

## Request prompt

In your game, when the last enemy of a wave dies, freeze the whole screen for 0.45 s: high-contrast monochrome, white radial lines from the impact, camera pushed in 6%; then restore colour and play the death effect.

## Implementation hint

Set gameplay timeScale = 0 while render continues; draw a fullscreen post pass (luminance threshold → 2-tone) and a radial-line overlay mesh. Same clock split as FB-10.
