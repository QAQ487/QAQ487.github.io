# UX-29 · Breadcrumbing / Breadcrumb trail

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

Coins, supplies, bloodstains, or traces of destruction form a followable sequence, with each next clue encouraging further exploration.

## Not to be confused with

This is not the website hierarchy navigation “Home > Equipment.” Players may only want the resources and turn back after collecting them; reaching the end is not guaranteed.

## Use it for

Coins, supplies, bloodstains, or traces of destruction form a followable sequence, with each next clue encouraging further exploration.

## Tunable parameters

trail item · spacing · visibility chain · reward value · alternate reading · endpoint

## Request prompt

Place 7 golden supplies along a 35 m side path, spaced 4–6 m apart so the next is visible from the previous one; end at a small chest, but allow players to turn back after collecting 3 and do not present this as website hierarchy breadcrumbs.

## Implementation hint

Place a sequence of cues so at least the next one is discoverable from each pickup or trace. Balance their spacing and value so they invite exploration without making completion mandatory or assuming collection guarantees forward movement.
