# A11Y-05 · Motion-sickness comfort anchors

**Aliases:** 玩了會暈; 3D 暈; 暈動症; Comfort mode; Motion sickness reduction; Fixed reference frame; Comfort vignette; 画面酔い対策

**Group:** Readability & accessibility
**Subcategory:** Visual modes & comfort

## What you see

During fast movement or camera turns the picture keeps a stable reference: a small dot fixed at the screen centre, a steady cockpit or HUD frame, or a temporary darkened, narrowed edge at high speed (comfort vignette). These fixed references give the eye something that does not move, reducing the conflict between vision and the sense of balance. Every option should be toggleable and adjustable in settings.

## Not to be confused with

Not the general option for less shaking and flashing (UX-20) and not the low-health vignette (FB-16, which signals danger rather than comfort). This entry is specifically about adding fixed references and speed-dependent field-of-view narrowing.

## Use it for

First-person games, driving and flying, VR, sections with fast dashes or large camera rotations.

## Tunable parameters

centre dot 3–6 px @ 40–80% · vignette max 20–40% · vignette fade 0.15–0.3 s · speed threshold · turn-speed cap · FOV slider (starting points)

## Request prompt

Add motion-sickness comfort settings: (1) an optional centre dot (4 px diameter, 60% opacity); (2) a comfort vignette that fades in over 0.2 s when movement or turn speed exceeds a threshold, covering at most 30% of the view (starting values); (3) a camera turn-speed cap and a field-of-view slider. Each can be toggled on its own, and they ship at low strength by default.

## Implementation hint

comfort = smoothstep(v0, v1, max(linSpeed/vRef, angSpeed/wRef)); vignetteRadius = lerp(1, 1-maxCover, comfort*strength) eased over fade time; draw as a soft radial mask in screen space after the HUD-free scene pass. Centre dot drawn last, unaffected by post-processing.
