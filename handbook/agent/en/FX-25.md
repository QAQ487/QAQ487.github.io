# FX-25 · Air Absorption / Distance Filtering

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Distant sounds are not only quieter; sharp high frequencies diminish, making the sound darker.

## Not to be confused with

An LPF is a filtering tool, not exclusively a “through-the-wall” effect. Distance absorption and occlusion can use similar processing, so check whether changes follow distance or obstacles.

## Use it for

Reinforce distance and distinguish distant sounds spectrally from nearby ones.

## Tunable parameters

distance range · low-pass cutoff · high-shelf gain · filter slope · smoothing time · dry level · obstacle override

## Request prompt

Add air absorption to distant gunfire: keep the low-pass cutoff at 18 kHz within 10 m, reduce it gradually to 4.5 kHz from 10–80 m with a 12 dB/oct slope, and smooth changes over 200 ms; do not reuse the distance parameter for wall occlusion.

## Implementation hint

Map distance to gradual high-frequency reduction in addition to level attenuation. Smooth filter changes and keep distance-driven filtering distinguishable from obstacle-driven occlusion in the control logic.
