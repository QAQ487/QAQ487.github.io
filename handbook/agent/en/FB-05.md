# FB-05 · Status effect tint

**Aliases:** Status colour overlay; Debuff tint; Elemental tint

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

While poisoned, burning, frozen or slowed, the character carries a translucent colour layer (green, orange, pale blue) for the whole duration, often with small particles or a pulse.

## Not to be confused with

Not a hit flash (FB-01, an instant full override) nor an aura (VFX-14, glow around the outside). Here the character itself changes colour and keeps it while the status lasts.

## Use it for

Tells at a glance who has which status; colours must match the element system.

## Tunable parameters

tint colour & strength 30–60% · pulse period · blend (lerp / multiply / additive) · paired particles · fade-out

## Request prompt

Add status tints to the game: poison overlays 40% green pulsing every 0.6 s with small green bubbles above the head; freeze overlays 50% pale blue and stops the animation; burn overlays 35% orange with sparks. Fade the tint out over 0.2 s when the status ends.

## Implementation hint

Same sprite shader as FB-01 with a second uniform: rgb = lerp(rgb, tintColor, tintAmount*(0.75+0.25*sin(time*pulse))). Multiply blending (rgb*tint) keeps shading readable for dark tints; lerp works better for ice/white.
