# UI-06 · Reward pop / Level-up fanfare

**Aliases:** Level-up burst; Reward popup; Celebration popup; Achievement pop

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** UI motion

## What you see

When the XP bar fills or a big reward is earned, large text pops up in the centre (e.g. "LEVEL UP!"): it grows from tiny to past full size and springs back, with rotating light rays and bursting star particles behind it; the level number bumps, and after 1–2 s it fades and the XP bar restarts from zero.

## Not to be confused with

Not a toast (UI-13, a small corner message that does not interrupt play); a reward pop is a deliberately loud celebration. Not a pickup sparkle (VFX-16, on an object in the world).

## Use it for

Level-ups, rare drops, achievements, stage clears, gacha results. Marks important moments, so keep it rare for it to carry weight.

## Tunable parameters

pop scale curve & peak · hold time · ray count & spin speed · particle count · sound sting sync · whether gameplay pauses

## Request prompt

In your game, play a level-up fanfare: when the XP bar fills, hold 0.1 s, then pop "LEVEL UP!" from 0% to 100% with ease-out back over 0.3 s (peaking around 125%); behind it 12 gold rays rotate at 30°/s and 20 gold star particles burst out; the level number in the top-left scales to 140% and increments; after 1.5 s fade everything out over 0.3 s and restart the XP bar from 0.

## Implementation hint

Draw on a top UI layer: rays as one additive textured quad rotated over time, text as a scaled quad with ease-out-back, particles via a small deterministic burst. Queue multiple rewards so they play one after another instead of overlapping. Use unscaled time if gameplay is paused.
