# FB-03 · Invincibility blink

**Aliases:** I-frame flicker; Invincibility flicker; Mercy invincibility; Damage blink

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

After taking damage the character blinks on and off (or flickers translucent) at a steady rhythm for about 1–2 s, during which it cannot be hurt again; then it returns to normal.

## Not to be confused with

Not a hit flash (FB-01, a single pulse). The blink rhythm itself is the timer for the remaining invulnerability, often speeding up near the end.

## Use it for

Prevents being juggled to death by a crowd and clearly signals "you are safe right now".

## Tunable parameters

i-frame duration 0.6–2 s · blink period · low alpha (0 vs 25%) · speed-up near end · whether attacks/collision also pass through

## Request prompt

In your game, give the hero 1.2 s of i-frames after taking damage: toggle the sprite between 100% and 25% opacity every 0.08 s, speeding up to every 0.04 s for the last 0.3 s, and ignore all damage checks during that window.

## Implementation hint

Purely gameplay-timer driven: alpha = (floor(time/period) % 2) ? lowAlpha : 1. Pass alpha via per-instance colour; enable alpha blending (SrcAlpha/InvSrcAlpha) for the sprite batch. Keep the damage-immune flag in the same component so visuals and logic cannot drift.
