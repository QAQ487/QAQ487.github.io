# FX-40 · Material-dependent footfall dust

**Aliases:** Surface interaction FX; Footstep dust; Ground contact particles; Material-based footstep VFX; 材質腳步塵; 足音の砂塵

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

A boot compresses dry earth and a low puff rolls away; the same step on clean stone produces none. Players often attribute this to weight. Short-lived material response — not a decal.

## Not to be confused with

Not persistent footprints (ENV-10) and not generic particles (FX-01) without a material branch. No emission in air or from an idle planted foot.

## Use it for

Realistic locomotion, floors that should answer footsteps.

## Tunable parameters

material table · speed scale · puff height · lifetime 0.3–1.2 s · plant vs scrape · no idle emit (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

On a grounded foot plant or scrape on an eligible material, emit a low surface-aligned puff whose amount follows impact/scrape speed and material; fine dust rises slower than grit; settle/fade in ~0.3–1.2 s.

## Implementation hint

OnFootPlant event with materialId + speed. Look up emit count/colour/gravity. Spawn a few billboards with mostly horizontal velocity and short life. Skip if !grounded or speed < eps. Contrast with ENV-10 decals in the same scene (DIRT vs STONE).
