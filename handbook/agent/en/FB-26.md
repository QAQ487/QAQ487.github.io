# FB-26 · Perfect parry flash / Just guard

**Aliases:** Perfect parry; Just guard; Deflect; Precision block; Parry flash; 完美格擋; 彈刀; 精準防禦; ジャストガード; 弾き; パリィ

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

The player presses guard in a tight window just before an enemy hit lands: a white-gold spark burst and an expanding ring pop at the weapon contact point, with a sharp, bright metallic ring while other sounds briefly duck. The pause is asymmetric — the defender freezes only for an instant and can act again, while the attacker freezes longer, then has the weapon knocked away and staggers back off-balance. Pressing early or late gives only a normal block: duller sparks, no ring, and the defender slides back instead.

## Not to be confused with

Not blockstun (ACT-23 — the time you cannot act after an ordinary block). Not a guard counter (ACT-42 — the special retaliate input after a successful block). Not a perfect dodge (ACT-43 — evasion, no contact). This card is the look, sound and asymmetric pause of the just-in-time block itself. Not a weapon clash (ACT-44 — both sides attacking, symmetric reaction).

## Use it for

Reading-based sword action, boss fights, defence systems that must make a normal block and a perfect block unmistakably different.

## Tunable parameters

perfect window (frames, authoring) · defender hitstop 2–4 f · attacker hitstop 8–12 f · spark colour / count · ring radius & life 0.15–0.25 s · audio duck depth & length · attacker recoil pose (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add a perfect-parry presentation to melee. Guard pressed inside a short window before the enemy hit resolves counts as perfect: spawn a white-gold spark burst and an expanding ring at the weapon contact point, play a sharp metallic ring and duck other audio for about 0.2 s; freeze the defender 2–4 frames and the attacker 8–12 frames, then knock the attacker's weapon away into a recoil pose. Guard outside the window plays the normal block (dull sparks, no ring, defender pushed back). Accept: blocking the same attack early, on time and late gives three clearly different looks and sounds.

## Implementation hint

Timestamp the guard input; when an incoming hit resolves, compare against it. Inside the perfect window raise a PerfectGuard event: spawn a spark burst and expanding ring at the weapon contact point, apply separate per-entity time-scale freezes (short for the defender, long for the attacker), push a brief duck on the audio mix, then put the attacker into a recoil / off-balance state. Outside the window route to normal block feedback.
