# FB-17 · Delayed health bar

**Aliases:** Ghost bar; Damage delay bar; Chip-damage trail; Catch-up bar

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** UI feedback

## What you see

On damage the bar’s foreground drops immediately, but a pale (white/yellow) ghost segment lingers before catching up, so the player can read how much was lost.

## Not to be confused with

Not a plain bar (instant drop), nor the "recoverable red health" mechanic of fighting games. The delayed bar is purely a display trail.

## Use it for

Makes each chunk of damage readable, especially rapid small hits; nearly every boss bar uses it.

## Tunable parameters

hold time 0.3–0.6 s · catch-up speed · ghost colour · heal preview colour · reset on new hit

## Request prompt

Use delayed health bars for the hero and bosses in the game: the red foreground updates instantly; the white ghost bar waits 0.4 s after damage, then catches up at 40% of max HP per second; for heals invert it — a green preview grows first and the foreground follows.

## Implementation hint

UI-only: two floats (shown, ghost). Draw three quads (bg, ghost, fg) in the UI batch; no special GPU work.
