# PP-18 · Image-intensifier night vision / NVG look

**Aliases:** Night vision; NVG look; Image-intensifier; White-phosphor NV; 微光夜視; 綠色夜視; 白磷夜視; 暗視ゴーグル; 微光暗視; ナイトビジョン

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

Darkness becomes a grainy green or white-phosphor monochrome image, with conspicuously sensitive bright lights (halos/bloom on lamps).

## Not to be confused with

REN-30 changes exposure. PP-02 adds grain. REN-32 changes colour. None alone names the amplified-light instrument picture. Unlike NAV-08, it need not identify clues or see through walls. Green is familiar, not mandatory; white-phosphor variants belong on the same card (param: phosphor palette).

## Use it for

Stealth, military, sci-fi goggle views.

## Tunable parameters

phosphor palette (green / white) · gain · noise · bloom on lights · HUD readability (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Switch night vision on in a dim room → scene view → reveal low-light structure in phosphor monochrome with restrained sensor noise and bright-source halos → restore ordinary visibility and colour on switch-off.

## Implementation hint

Luminance → phosphor tint (green or white-hot grey), add mild grain, bloom bright practicals. Optionally lift near-black gain. Keep readable HUD outside or lightly treated.
