# UX-04 · Objective marker / Waypoint；Objective tracker

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** On-screen Information and Menus

## What you see

A floating diamond in the distance with a distance readout is a marker/waypoint; a side-of-screen instruction such as “Activate three generators 1/3” is a tracker. The former answers “Where do I go?” and the latter “What do I do?”.  Markers can also pin the aimed world point under a reticle/scope (far pins), kept in sync with UI-08 minimap icons.

## Not to be confused with

A marker over a destination does not provide a traversable route by itself; an icon visible through a wall may not tell you where the entrance is.

## Tunable parameters

world marker · distance · objective text · progress count · occlusion · route context

## Request prompt

Build objective guidance with a world marker labeled Generator at 142 m and a side tracker reading Activate generators 1/3; make the marker hollow through walls and show an entrance cue only within 10 m rather than implying a straight traversable route.

## Implementation hint

Treat the waypoint and objective tracker as separate components backed by the same objective state. Clamp off-screen markers, state whether they are occluded, and avoid presenting a straight-line marker as a traversable route.
