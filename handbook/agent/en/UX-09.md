# UX-09 · Progressive disclosure

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** On-screen Information and Menus

## What you see

A settings page initially shows volume and subtitles; less frequently used options appear only after selecting “Advanced.” Details are revealed as needed instead of being presented all at once.

## Not to be confused with

Hiding important features is not automatically simplicity. A mandatory first, second, and third step that everyone must complete is closer to staged disclosure, which is not the same thing.

## Use it for

A settings page initially shows volume and subtitles; less frequently used options appear only after selecting “Advanced.” Details are revealed as needed instead of being presented all at once.

## Tunable parameters

primary settings · advanced section · disclosure control · persisted state · search · importance

## Request prompt

Organize a settings page with 3 first-level controls—master volume, subtitles, and text size—and reveal 5 less-used options under Advanced Audio; never hide subtitles, let a search for night find the concealed option, and preserve expansion for the session.

## Implementation hint

Show frequent and safety-critical controls at the first level, with optional details behind a clearly labeled disclosure control. Preserve the expanded state when useful and ensure hidden options remain discoverable through labels or search.
