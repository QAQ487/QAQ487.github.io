# FX-35 · Multisensory Redundancy

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Haptics, Accessibility, and Cross-sensory Information

## What you see

The same hazard has a clear graphic, sound, and optional vibration. With sound or vibration disabled, other ways to understand the event remain.

## Not to be confused with

Color plus shape is still entirely visual and does not by itself accommodate blind players. Making everything stronger at once is not multisensory clarity either.

## Use it for

Avoid requiring players to depend on one sense or one device type.

## Tunable parameters

visual cue · audio cue · haptic cue · channel toggle · semantic match · timing tolerance · fallback audit

## Request prompt

For a hazard that explodes in one second, provide a red-and-white boundary graphic, a warning tone accelerating every 250 ms, and an optional three-pulse vibration, with channel onsets within 50 ms; test each sound-off, haptics-off, and visual-assist-off combination for complete information.

## Implementation hint

Encode the same event meaning across independent sensory channels with closely aligned timing and consistent urgency. Audit every channel-off combination so no required information depends on one sense or device.
