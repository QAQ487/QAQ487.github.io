# UX-33 · Soft gate / Soft gating

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Access Rules, Returning, and Learning

## What you see

An exit mechanism must be operated under enemy fire. In theory you can rush it, but normally you clear the area first. Pressure, rather than an absolute lock, constrains movement flow.

## Not to be confused with

Not every difficult enemy is a soft gate; check whether it actually functions to block passage/progression. Industry usage also extends soft gating more broadly to dangerous areas.

## Use it for

An exit mechanism must be operated under enemy fire. In theory you can rush it, but normally you clear the area first. Pressure, rather than an absolute lock, constrains movement flow.

## Tunable parameters

pressure source · traversal cost · bypass chance · risk signal · alternate preparation · progression role

## Request prompt

Design a soft gate where an exit control requires a 4-second hold under fire from 3 enemies; skilled players may rush it using 2 smoke grenades while most clear the room first, and the UI should signal risk without locking the exit absolutely.

## Implementation hint

Create a passage that remains technically possible while imposing a meaningful cost or risk that usually encourages preparation or delay. Confirm that the pressure actually regulates access, rather than labeling any difficult encounter a soft gate.
