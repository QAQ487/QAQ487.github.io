# UX-10 · Subtitles / Closed captions（CC）

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** On-screen Information and Menus

## What you see

Displaying dialogue alone is a common form of subtitles. Adding information such as “footsteps behind you,” “alarm sounds,” and speaker identification falls within the scope of captions, helping players understand cues they cannot hear.

## Not to be confused with

Every sound need not be transcribed individually; the priority is not to omit sounds that affect understanding or decisions. Product interfaces sometimes use these names interchangeably, so inspect what is actually included.

## Use it for

Displaying dialogue alone is a common form of subtitles. Adding information such as “footsteps behind you,” “alarm sounds,” and speaker identification falls within the scope of captions, helping players understand cues they cannot hear.

## Tunable parameters

speaker label · dialogue · sound cue · direction · line length · display duration

## Request prompt

Design subtitles and closed captions with at most 42 characters per line, 2 lines, and a 2-second minimum duration; identify speakers, caption important audio as [Footsteps behind] with direction, and omit 10 seconds of irrelevant wind ambience.

## Implementation hint

Build captions from timed dialogue and meaningful non-speech events, with optional speaker and direction metadata. Limit line length, guarantee sufficient reading time, and omit ambient sounds that do not affect understanding or decisions.
