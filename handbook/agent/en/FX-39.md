# FX-39 · Sound concurrency / voice stealing

**Aliases:** 聲音併發上限; 語音搶佔; Voice stealing; Audio voice limit; Polyphony limit; ボイススティール

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Only N sounds play at once; when full, lower-priority, farther or older voices are stopped so new ones can start. Busy scenes stay readable instead of turning to mush.

## Not to be confused with

Not only volume ducking — stealing stops or truncates an old voice to insert a new one.

## Use it for

Dense gunfire/explosions, many barks, UI vs world audio contention.

## Tunable parameters

max voices · priority layers · distance weight · non-stealable flags (starting points)

## Request prompt

Cap voices (e.g. 32); each voice has priority and stealThreshold. When full, stop the worst candidate if the new sound outranks it. Mission dialogue gets max priority and non-stealable.

## Implementation hint

Voice pool: OnPlay find free or stealLowest(score=priority - distWeight*dist - ageBias). Stop stolen with short fade.
