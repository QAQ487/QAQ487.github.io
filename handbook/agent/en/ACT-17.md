# ACT-17 · Staging / Pose Readability

**Group:** Animation, movement & physics
**Subcategory:** Animation Principles and Action Readability

## What you see

When an enemy raises a weapon, its silhouette, the camera, and the background make the coming downward strike immediately clear. The opposite happens when the body or effects obscure the weapon.

## Not to be confused with

Simply making a scene attractive. Extra flashes and camera shake can reduce readability. Staging also encompasses more than one character's pose.

## Use it for

Direct attention toward important actions, helping players read attacks and understand spatial relationships.

## Tunable parameters

silhouette separation · camera angle · contrast · effect density · attack direction

## Request prompt

Stage the enemy's slash anticipation with at least 12 px of silhouette separation between weapon and torso, 25% background luminance contrast, and half the nonessential effects during the 250 ms tell.

## Implementation hint

Arrange pose, camera, background contrast, and effects so the important action reads as a distinct silhouette. Remove or redirect elements that obscure the weapon path or intended target.
