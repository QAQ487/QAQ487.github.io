# FB-08 · Camera kick

**Aliases:** Recoil kick; Directional screen shake; Camera recoil

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Camera feedback

## What you see

On firing or a heavy hit the camera jolts in one specific direction (usually opposite the attack) and springs back quickly — a single, directional push.

## Not to be confused with

Not omnidirectional random shake (FB-07) nor a punch-in (FB-09, which changes zoom). A kick only translates, with a clear direction.

## Use it for

Shooting, charged attacks, heavy knockback — it makes the direction of force felt.

## Tunable parameters

kick distance · direction · spring stiffness/damping · overshoot · clamp for rapid fire

## Request prompt

In your game, when the hero swings a heavy hammer or fires an arrow, offset the camera 3 px opposite the attack and spring it back within 0.12 s with slight overshoot; during rapid fire, add per shot but clamp the total at 6 px.

## Implementation hint

Damped spring on a camera offset vector (x += v*dt; v += (-k*x - c*v)*dt). Add an impulse to v on each event. Combine with trauma shake by summing offsets before the view matrix.
