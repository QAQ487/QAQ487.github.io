# FB-20 · Aim-assist friction and magnetism

**Aliases:** 手把瞄準輔助; 準星黏住敵人; 瞄準變慢; Aim assist; Aim friction / slowdown; Aim magnetism; Sticky aim; エイムアシスト

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Aiming & handling

## What you see

When aiming with a controller, the reticle slows slightly as it crosses an enemy (friction), making it easier to stay on target; when the target moves, the reticle follows it a little (magnetism). The ideal is that players feel they aimed well rather than being auto-locked. Strength should be adjustable or disableable, and configured separately per input device.

## Not to be confused with

Not a lock-on camera (CAMX-03, where the camera points straight at the target) and not pickup magnetism (FB-14, items flying to the player). Aim assist only adjusts the player's input; it does not replace it.

## Use it for

Controller or touch shooters, ranged attacks in action games, aim-assist strength in accessibility settings.

## Tunable parameters

friction zone 1.1–1.5× target bounds · friction scale 0.4–0.8 · magnetism 0–30% of target velocity · only with stick input · per-device strength 0–100% (starting points)

## Request prompt

Add controller aim assist: when the reticle is within 1.2× of a target's bounds, multiply stick turn rate by 0.5 (friction, starting value); when the target moves, carry the reticle 20% of the target's velocity along its direction (magnetism, starting value), only while the player is actively pushing the stick. Provide a 0–100% strength slider and an off switch; leave it off by default for mouse input.

## Implementation hint

Each frame: find nearest target in screen space; if inside zone: turn *= lerp(1, frictionScale, strength*falloff); if stick magnitude > deadzone: aim += targetScreenVel*magnetism*strength*dt. Never rotate without input; clamp so assist cannot exceed player turn speed.
