# UX-30 · Lighting cues / Color coding

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

An exit is illuminated in a dark room, or different areas use consistent palettes to help players recognize entrances and their current location.

## Not to be confused with

“The brightest spot is the exit” is not a universal rule; artistic atmosphere can complicate interpretation. Essential directions should not rely solely on red/green distinctions either.

## Use it for

An exit is illuminated in a dark room, or different areas use consistent palettes to help players recognize entrances and their current location.

## Tunable parameters

exit lighting · area palette · symbol · luminance contrast · color-blind support · atmosphere

## Request prompt

Give 3 areas stable palettes and mark exits with doorway lighting at 4.5:1 contrast against the background; also use a plus for Medical and a triangle for Hangar, avoiding red/green-only coding and misleading brighter decorative lights.

## Implementation hint

Use stable lighting and palette relationships to distinguish areas and emphasize relevant entrances, then reinforce them with shape, labels, or symbols. Verify luminance and context because the brightest object is not inherently the destination.
