# FB-09 · Camera punch-in

**Aliases:** Impact zoom; Zoom punch; Hit zoom

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Camera feedback

## What you see

On a heavy hit the camera zooms in a few percent quickly (or toward the impact point), then eases back to the original zoom over a fraction of a second.

## Not to be confused with

Not slow motion (FB-10, time slows) nor camera kick (FB-08, translation). A punch-in changes zoom to emphasise focus.

## Use it for

Boss blows, charged slashes, final hits; stronger combined with a freeze frame.

## Tunable parameters

peak zoom 104–115% · attack time · release time & curve · zoom pivot (screen centre vs impact point)

## Request prompt

In your game, when a charged heavy attack lands, zoom the camera to 108% centred on the impact point within 0.05 s, then ease out back to 100% over 0.35 s; in pixel mode either use integer steps or accept non-integer zoom only during the punch.

## Implementation hint

Scale the orthographic projection (or the low-res → backbuffer blit rect) around a pivot. For pixel-perfect output, render the low-res target slightly larger and crop, or accept a short non-integer phase with point sampling.
