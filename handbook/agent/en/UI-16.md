# UI-16 · Dialogue box / Text box

**Aliases:** Text box; Message window; Speech box; Dialogue window

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** Text & dialogue

## What you see

A wide box appears at the bottom (or top) of the screen holding the dialogue text, with the speaker’s name tag on or beside its edge and often a portrait; it opens with a short expanding animation, the name tag and portrait switch sides when the speaker changes, and the box closes when the conversation ends.

## Not to be confused with

Not a speech bubble (CIN-06, attached to a character in the world with a tail pointing at the speaker); a dialogue box is fixed on screen, and portrait expressions and mouth flaps are covered by CIN-05. Not the typewriter effect (UI-15, only how the text appears). Not a tooltip (UI-12, appears when pointing at an object).

## Use it for

Story conversations, NPC chats, tutorials, system messages; keeps "who is speaking and what they say" in one readable place.

## Tunable parameters

position & size · open/close animation · portrait size & side · name-tag style · lines per page · advance indicator · auto vs manual advance

## Request prompt

Add a dialogue box to your game: fixed at the bottom with 8 px side margins and 40 px height, drawn with nine-slice; open it from 0% height with ease-out back over 0.15 s; a 32×32 px portrait on the left with a name tag above it (speaker name in the character’s theme colour); when the speaker changes, fade portrait and tag across to the other side over 0.1 s; at most 3 lines per page, revealed with a typewriter effect; close over 0.1 s when the conversation ends.

## Implementation hint

A UI-layer widget: nine-slice frame (UI-14) + portrait sprite + name label + text block. Drive it from a dialogue queue of {speaker, side, text}. Animate the frame’s height with a tween (UI-01) and clip the text while it is opening so glyphs are not squashed.
