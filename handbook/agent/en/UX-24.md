# UX-24 · Signposting / In-world signage

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

An arrow labeled “Medical” at a junction or an area name above a doorway helps players choose a direction when needed or recognize that they have arrived.

## Not to be confused with

Signposting is sometimes used broadly for many kinds of guidance cues; signage in the narrower sense refers to actual signs. A sign in a scene is not necessarily read as useful information: players may consider it decoration.

## Use it for

An arrow labeled “Medical” at a junction or an area name above a doorway helps players choose a direction when needed or recognize that they have arrived.

## Tunable parameters

junction · arrow · destination label · arrival sign · readability distance · visual hierarchy

## Request prompt

Place a directional sign 8 m before a T-junction, with Medical 40 m to the left and Hangar 65 m to the right; add 1 arrival label above each doorway, readable from 10 m and visually distinct from 6 decorative posters.

## Implementation hint

Place explicit signs where players make route decisions and confirmation signs where they arrive. Make destinations readable before the decision point and visually distinguish operational signage from decorative set dressing.
