# FX-08 · Telegraph; Area-of-effect Indicator (Related Concepts, Not Synonyms)

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

A telegraph is a recognizable cue before an action occurs. It can be a raised sword, a preparatory sound, or an aiming line—not just a ground circle. An area-of-effect indicator communicates the affected area; it can also mark an already active damage zone or an allied healing area, so it is not necessarily a pre-attack warning. Circles, lines, or sector boundaries appear before an attack takes effect, helping you predict the dangerous location and direction and watch for when to dodge.

## Not to be confused with

An attractive magic circle may be purely decorative. A warning should match the actual area, timing, and degree of avoidability; a circle does not guarantee that you can dodge.

## Use it for

Give avoidable attacks a readable decision window.

## Tunable parameters

warning duration · area shape · radius · active duration · boundary width · timing cue · team state

## Request prompt

Add a 1.2-second telegraph for a 4 m radius ground slam: show a stable circular boundary for 0.9 seconds, pulse it four times per second during the final 0.3 seconds, then change it to a distinct 2-second active-damage state after impact.

## Implementation hint

Drive the warning cue and the affected-area display from the same gameplay geometry and timing data. Distinguish the pre-action telegraph from any area that remains active after impact through animation, labeling, or state change.
