# UX-20 · Reduced motion / Motion settings

**Aliases:** Reduced motion; 減少動態; Motion settings; Hit outline without shake

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** Motion and Interaction Feedback

## What you see

Disabling animated menu backgrounds, reducing camera shake/head bob, or stopping automatic scrolling helps players concentrate on reading information clearly.

## Not to be confused with

This is not a low-graphics-quality mode, nor does it necessarily remove all in-game movement. Focus on nonessential motion, camera effects, and reading burden.

## Use it for

Disabling animated menu backgrounds, reducing camera shake/head bob, or stopping automatic scrolling helps players concentrate on reading information clearly. With camera shake or motion blur off, a hit still needs a stable short outline and a short sound, and aiming and hazard positions stay visible. (Former entry FX-37, “Reduced motion”, is merged here.) Prefer a tiny roll (~±0.3°) or viewmodel-only bob over vertical eye-height oscillation; expose bob: off / subtle / full. Idle camera breathing is CAM-33.

## Tunable parameters

camera shake · head bob · comfort sway (tiny roll ~±0.3° or viewmodel-only) · bob preset off/subtle/full · menu motion · auto-scroll · flashing · intensity preset

## Request prompt

Add camera bob: off / subtle / full. Prefer a tiny roll (~±0.3°) or viewmodel-only bob over vertical eye-height oscillation; scale with speed and ease out on stop. Naive vertical bob is a common motion-sickness cause. Idle camera breathing is CAM-33 — keep it separate from this locomotion comfort sway.

## Implementation hint

Expose independent controls for nonessential camera, interface, scrolling, and flashing effects, plus a reduced-motion preset. Prefer comfort sway — tiny roll (~±0.3°) or viewmodel-only bob scaled with speed and eased out on stop — over vertical eye-height oscillation, which commonly causes motion sickness. Idle camera breathing (CAM-33) is a separate idle effect.
