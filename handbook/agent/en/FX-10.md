# FX-10 · Shape Language

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Without relying on color, you can recognize silhouettes such as “long and pointed forward,” “spreading circle,” or “a shell surrounding the character.” Similar effects follow consistent shape rules.

## Not to be confused with

“Points always mean enemies; circles always mean safety” is not a universal rule. Read the visual vocabulary established by the particular game.

## Use it for

Help players quickly infer direction, coverage, and state categories.

## Tunable parameters

silhouette family · directionality · curvature · aspect ratio · enclosure · repetition · grayscale readability

## Request prompt

Define three ability-shape families: a forward-pointing 5:1 form for linear attacks, a complete ring for radial bursts, and a closed shell around the character for protection; create four variants of each that remain distinguishable in grayscale.

## Implementation hint

Define a small, consistent silhouette vocabulary for direction, spread, enclosure, and state. Validate each family without color and teach its meaning through repeated use within the same product rather than relying on universal symbolism.
