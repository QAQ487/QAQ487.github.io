# FX-01 · Sprite / Billboard Particles

**Aliases:** Soft particles; Depth fade; GPU particles; 柔和粒子; 煙邊硬邊

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Smoke, sparks, and points of light consist of many small textured planes. As the camera turns, these planes may keep facing it. Not all sprites are locked to the camera; they can also align with velocity.

## Not to be confused with

Apparent volume does not prove the use of a 3D mesh. Conversely, lighting and textures can give a single plane an impression of thickness.

## Use it for

Combine simple carriers into smoke, magical glints, and dust. Smoke sprites should fade as they meet the ground or a wall, with no straight hard seam. A large field of sparks or dust is still these sprites; which processor runs the simulation is not visible. (Former entries VFX-01, “Soft particles”, and VFX-02, “GPU particles”, are merged here.)

## Tunable parameters

spawn rate · sprite size · facing mode · velocity alignment · lifetime · opacity curve

## Request prompt

Create a 2.5-second magical smoke puff that emits 40 translucent planes per second; face 70% toward the camera, align 30% to velocity, and grow each from 0.15 m to 0.6 m while fading out.

## Implementation hint

Emit textured planes and choose camera-facing, axis-locked, or velocity-aligned orientation per effect. Vary size, rotation, and opacity over lifetime so the group creates volume without implying physical geometry. Fade a sprite as it approaches a surface so smoke meets the ground or a wall without a hard edge.
