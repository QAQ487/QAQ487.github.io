# FX-24 · Directivity / Attenuation Shape

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

At the same distance, a loudspeaker is clear in front and weaker to the side or rear; alternatively, pipe noise extends along an elongated region.

## Not to be confused with

Emission direction is a source property; panning places sound in the listener’s output. They are different. A box-shaped sound region does not imply simulated room reflections.

## Use it for

Express loudspeaker orientation, elongated sources, and room ambience regions.

## Tunable parameters

emission cone · rear attenuation · source length · attenuation volume · orientation · edge blend · listener distance

## Request prompt

Give a loudspeaker a 60° inner cone and 120° outer cone: hold 0 dB in front, reduce the side to −9 dB and the rear to −18 dB, smooth transitions over 15° at cone boundaries, and test at a fixed 5 m distance.

## Implementation hint

Model emission direction or spatial extent independently from output panning. Blend smoothly across cone or volume boundaries and use elongated or box regions only when they match the source’s functional shape.
