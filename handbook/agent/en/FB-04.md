# FB-04 · Death effect

**Aliases:** Death burst; Death poof; Defeat effect

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

A defeated enemy does not just vanish: it flashes, swells, then pops into chunks, smoke or pixel particles, possibly leaving loot or a corpse.

## Not to be confused with

Not an explosion (VFX-09, with fireball and shock) nor a dissolve (VFX-04, fading along a noisy edge). A death effect is the closing beat that says "this enemy is definitely done".

## Use it for

Gives kills a clear ending and satisfaction; pairs with pickups (FB-14) and permanence (FB-12).

## Tunable parameters

pre-flash time · swell scale · chunk count & speed · gravity · smoke lifetime · loot delay

## Request prompt

In your game, when an enemy dies: flash white for 0.06 s and scale to 120%, then split into 10–14 green/dark-green pixel chunks that burst outward and fall under gravity, plus a grey-white smoke puff fading over 0.4 s; finally drop a coin at the spot.

## Implementation hint

CPU-side burst emitter writing into the shared sprite/particle dynamic vertex buffer; chunks are 1–2 px quads sampled from the dying sprite palette. Optionally use the sprite texture itself split into tiles for real chunks.
