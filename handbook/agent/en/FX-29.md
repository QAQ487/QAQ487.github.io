# FX-29 · Adaptive Music: Vertical Layering

**Aliases:** Adaptive Music

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

The same passage and beat continue while drums or bass enter during combat and some instruments drop out in calm periods.

## Not to be confused with

This is not merely turning up a song. The combination or balance of synchronized tracks changes. It can also be combined with the next technique.

## Use it for

Maintain musical continuity while reflecting tension or game state.

## Tunable parameters

synchronized stems · intensity value · layer thresholds · crossfade bars · phase lock · gain matrix · exit hysteresis

## Request prompt

Split a 120 BPM score into four synchronized stems—drums, bass, harmony, and melody; add bass above 40% threat and drums above 70%, crossfade each change over two bars, and remove a layer only after threat falls 10% below its threshold.

## Implementation hint

Keep musical stems synchronized to a shared timeline and vary their gains or participation according to state. Quantize or smooth changes so layers enter musically without replacing the current passage.
