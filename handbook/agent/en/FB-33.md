# FB-33 · Telekinetic grab & throw

**Aliases:** Telekinesis; Telekinetic throw; Gravity gun; Kinesis grab; Psychokinetic launch; Catch and release; Catch and throw back; 念力抓物投擲; 重力槍; 接住擲回; 念動力投げ; キャッチ＆リリース; Magnetic grab; Magnesis; 磁力抓取; マグネキャッチ

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

The player aims at a prop in the scene (crate, rubble, barrel) and holds the ability button: the prop tears free, flies along a short arc to a point in front of and above the player, and hovers there with a slight spin and wobble, a ring of fine particles around it and a tether line to the hand. The reticle switches to a throw reticle. On release the prop shoots out straight along the aim with a trail, shattering on impact with an enemy or wall and knocking the target back. Variant — catch and release: a grenade or rocket thrown by an enemy is grabbed in flight by the same ability, held in front of the player while switching to the player's colour, and thrown back with another press. Variant — magnetic grab: only metal objects can be grabbed; when the ability is on they light up in a distinct colour while everything else stays as it is, and a grabbed metal object follows the reticle and can be used to push or smash into things.

## Not to be confused with

Not projectile batting (FB-32, sent straight back within one swing, never held). Not weapon recall (FB-30, a fixed weapon flying back to the hand). Not pickup magnetism (FB-14, loot flies to the player and disappears). Telekinesis is the three-step grab → hold ready → aimed throw.

## Use it for

Psychic powers, gravity guns, magnetic gloves; turning clutter into ammunition; defusing enemy grenade pressure.

## Tunable parameters

grab range 10–15 m & reticle cone · pull time 0.2–0.35 s & arc height · hold point offset (front / above) · hover spin & bob · tether & particle ring · launch speed & trail · catch window for incoming throws · team colour swap on catch (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add telekinetic grab and throw. While the ability button is held, pick the best grabbable prop within 10–15 m near the reticle: it flies along an arc over 0.2–0.35 s to a hold point about 1–1.5 m in front of and above the player, keeps a slight spin and bob while held, with a particle ring and a tether line to the hand. On release it launches fast along the aim with a trail and shatters on impact, knocking the target back. Variant: objects thrown by enemies can also be grabbed in flight; while held they switch to the player's colour and are thrown back on the throw input. Accept: grab, hold and throw read as three distinct stages, and both a floor prop and an enemy grenade caught mid-air hover and throw correctly.

## Implementation hint

Pick the best grabbable within a cone and range around the reticle. Switch it to kinematic and move it along a short arc to a hold socket in front of the camera; while held, spring it toward the socket with a little noise for wobble and spin. On release restore physics, set its velocity along the aim ray (or toward an aim-assisted target), enable a trail and resolve impact with a knockback impulse. For catch and release, let incoming thrown projectiles be grabbable in flight: suspend their fuse damage to the player, switch team and colour, and hold them like any other prop.
