# FX-30 · Adaptive Music: Horizontal Resequencing

**Aliases:** Adaptive Music

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

An exploration passage transitions into a combat passage, followed by another short segment. The change concerns what plays next, not just which instruments join.

## Not to be confused with

Random song playback does not necessarily provide musical transitions. Horizontal resequencing requires attention to segments and transition arrangements; it does not require an immediate switch at every possible moment.

## Use it for

Change segment order according to state and create different musical paths.

## Tunable parameters

segment graph · transition marker · bar length · minimum stay · state condition · bridge segment · fallback path

## Request prompt

Build exploration, combat, and victory segments for a 100 BPM score; after a state change, transition no later than the next four-bar boundary, use a one-bar bridge from exploration to combat, and require an eight-bar minimum stay for ordinary switches.

## Implementation hint

Represent music as segments connected by musically valid transition points and select the next segment from gameplay state. Respect bar or phrase boundaries and provide bridge and fallback routes for state changes.
