# FX-11 · Primary / Secondary Elements

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

A clear main form registers first, followed by supporting sparks, thin smoke, and other details. The main information survives when the image is reduced in size.

## Not to be confused with

More detail does not necessarily mean a more finished effect. When everything is equally bright and fast, it becomes harder to know where to look.

## Use it for

Prioritize hit boundaries, direction, and focal points without letting decoration dominate.

## Tunable parameters

primary silhouette · secondary count · size ratio · luminance ratio · onset order · detail density · thumbnail scale

## Request prompt

Build a heavy strike around one primary arc occupying about 70% of the effect area, then add at most 12 sparks and two thin smoke layers; keep supporting peak luminance below 60% of the main form and preserve attack direction at 25% display size.

## Implementation hint

Build the effect around one dominant form that communicates direction or coverage, then add lower-contrast supporting details. Check the result at reduced size and remove details that compete with the primary message.
