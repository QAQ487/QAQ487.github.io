# FB-12 · Permanence

**Aliases:** Persistent debris; Corpses & decals; Leftover shells

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

Traces of combat do not vanish immediately: corpses, shells, blood or scorch marks and debris stay on the ground, so the arena remembers what happened.

## Not to be confused with

Not debris flying out (VFX-18, the moment of ejection); permanence is the state that remains afterwards.

## Use it for

Strengthens cause-and-effect and a sense of place; both "Juice it or lose it" and Nijman list it as a game-feel trick.

## Tunable parameters

max count · lifetime / never · fade-out · layer (below characters) · variety

## Request prompt

In your game, leave a dark scorch decal and 2–3 small bone sprites where each enemy dies; keep at most 64 and fade the oldest over 1 s when exceeded. Draw them above the ground and below characters.

## Implementation hint

Ring buffer of decal instances drawn in a dedicated layer between ground tiles and Y-sorted sprites; one instanced draw. For huge counts, bake decals into a persistent render target over the ground.
