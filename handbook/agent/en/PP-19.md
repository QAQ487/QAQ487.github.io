# PP-19 · Thermal vision / Thermographic imaging

**Aliases:** Thermal vision; FLIR-style; White-hot; Black-hot; Thermographic; 熱成像; 熱能視覺; 白熱／黑熱; 熱画像; サーモグラフィ; ホワイトホット

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

Bodies and recently heated objects stand out according to *heat*, in white-hot, black-hot, or a false-colour thermal palette.

## Not to be confused with

REN-32 recolours by tone. NAV-08 marks semantic clues. MATX-10 shows occluded silhouettes. Thermal presentation derives hierarchy from heat — not visible brightness, enemy identity, or automatic wall penetration. Acceptance must include a visibly dark but hot object (e.g. a black kettle or just-fired barrel); otherwise a luminance gradient can masquerade as thermal vision.

## Use it for

Stealth scans, sci-fi sensors, search and rescue.

## Tunable parameters

palette (white-hot / black-hot / ironbow) · body heat · prop heat · cool rate · wall penetration off by default (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Enable thermal view → visible scene surfaces → map cool surroundings and warm bodies/barrels to a temperature palette; let a hot object cool visibly → return to normal view on disable.

## Implementation hint

Author a heat buffer (not copy of luminance): warm bodies/barrels high, cold lights low even if optically bright. Remap heat through a thermal LUT. Animate cooling. Do not auto-see through walls unless a separate gameplay rule says so.
