# UX-40 · Revive prompt

**Aliases:** 復活提示; 救助互動; Help up prompt; Revive hold; 蘇生プロンプト

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** Multiplayer cues

## What you see

Near a downed ally a “hold to revive” prompt and progress ring appear; completing the hold revives them, releasing or taking damage cancels or drains progress.

## Not to be confused with

Not a generic interact prompt alone (NAV-01) — it carries progress and multiplayer state. Not objective voting (UX-41).

## Use it for

Co-op downed allies; assault-style modes.

## Tunable parameters

range · hold duration · progress drain on hit · revive HP% (starting points)

## Request prompt

Show the prompt within 2.5 m; a 1.5–2.5 s hold fills the ring and revives at ~30% HP (starting values). Leaving range or taking a hit clears progress in 0.3 s.

## Implementation hint

while Hold && inRange && !interrupted: p+=dt/dur; on p>=1 Revive(). On hit: p=0.
