# FB-37 · Object rewind

**Aliases:** Recall; Local time reversal; Rewind a single object; Object time reverse; Reverse trajectory; Time restore; 物件倒帶; 物件時間倒流; 局部倒轉; 軌跡倒退; オブジェクト巻き戻し; 逆再生; 時間逆行

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Time manipulation

## What you see

When the player activates the ability, the world darkens under a colour tint and only the objects that can be rewound light up with an outline. Picking one (a rock that has just fallen, say, or a crate that dropped into the water) shows its past path as a string of translucent ghost copies joined by a dotted line. On confirm the object starts to go backwards: it moves and turns in reverse along its original path, the rock flying back up to the top of the cliff, while the other objects and characters around it carry on as normal. While it rewinds the character can stand on it and ride along, and the player can stop it at any moment, leaving the object where it is. When it reaches the start of its record or the time runs out, the object stops, the ghosts vanish and the colours come back.

## Not to be confused with

Not a global rewind (the whole world, player included, running backwards, as when undoing a mistake). Not stasis with stored impact (FB-31, which only freezes an object and stores hits). Not weapon recall (FB-30, where the weapon flies straight from where it is to the hand rather than retracing its past path). Not slow motion (FB-10).

## Use it for

Physics puzzles (rewinding a fallen platform so it works as a lift), sending enemy projectiles back the way they came, restoring broken objects, time-themed adventures.

## Tunable parameters

rewindable tag · history length & sampling · select-mode look (darken, tint, outline) · ghost spacing & path line · rewind speed · carries riders · stop at any time · end: back to physics, clear ghosts & filter (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add object rewind. Every rewindable object keeps recording its recent position and rotation. On activating the ability, apply a dark, tinted screen filter and outline the rewindable objects; for the selected one, show its past path as translucent ghosts and a dotted line. On confirm, play the recorded positions and rotations back in reverse with physics off, letting characters stand on it, while everything else keeps simulating. The player can stop it at any time; at the start of the record or when time runs out, stop the object, give it back to physics and remove the ghosts and filter. Accept: the rewound path lies on top of the original path, and a character standing on the object is carried smoothly without passing through it.

## Implementation hint

Ring-buffer each rewindable body's transform at a fixed sample rate. In select mode, apply a world tint and outline the rewindables; for the chosen one, draw ghost copies at spaced history samples and a polyline through them. On rewind, switch the body to kinematic and step the playback index backwards over time, interpolating between samples; move riders with the platform's per-step delta so they ride smoothly. On stop, or at the start of the buffer, return it to dynamic with zero velocity and clear the trail.
