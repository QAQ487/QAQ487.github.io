# CAM-25 · Close-up／CU

**Group:** Camera & composition
**Subcategory:** Composition and Cinematics

## What you see

A face, held object, or important detail occupies most of the frame, greatly reducing surrounding information. (A general shot-size term.)

## Not to be confused with

A close-up does not necessarily enable DOF or use a long focal length. It describes framing scale, not a unique set of camera parameters.

## Use it for

Reveals subtle expressions and emphasizes props or emotion at moments requiring special player attention.

## Tunable parameters

subject screen coverage · framing target · camera distance/FOV · entry duration · hold time · exit transition · headroom

## Request prompt

In your game, add a close-up when the player obtains the key: transition to the hand over 0.3 s, framing the key at 62% of screen height and viewport (0.5, 0.46). Hold for 1.1 s, then return to gameplay over 0.4 s. Preserve the existing depth-of-field settings instead of adding blur automatically.

## Implementation hint

Define the close-up by projected subject coverage and framing anchor, then solve distance or FOV according to scene constraints. Keep depth of field and focal length as independent artistic choices rather than implicitly enabling them with the shot scale.
