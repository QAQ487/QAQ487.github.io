# UX-36 · Unlockable shortcut / Loopback

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Access Rules, Returning, and Learning

## What you see

After a long detour, you open the lobby’s iron gate from behind or lower a ladder. Later visits no longer require the long route, and the connection helps you understand how areas fit together.

## Not to be confused with

A route is not an unlockable shortcut merely because it is short. The key feature is that it was blocked and is unlocked from the other side. A shortcut is not fast-travel teleportation either.

## Use it for

After a long detour, you open the lobby’s iron gate from behind or lower a ladder. Later visits no longer require the long route, and the connection helps you understand how areas fit together.

## Tunable parameters

initial blockage · far-side unlock · loopback · travel reduction · spatial reveal · persistent state

## Request prompt

Show a locked iron gate from the lobby, requiring a 180 m route to reach and open its far side; afterward reduce travel between the areas to 25 m, update the map connection, persist it, and do not substitute fast travel or an always-open short path.

## Implementation hint

Present the shortcut as blocked on first contact, then allow it to be opened only after reaching the far side through the long route. Persist the change and make the new connection legible as part of the area’s topology, not as teleportation.
