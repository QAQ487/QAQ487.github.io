# UX-18 · Multimodal feedback / Redundant cues

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** Motion and Interaction Feedback

## What you see

A new objective comes with text, a symbol, and a sound; danger comes with audio, a directional marker, and vibration. Players need not rely solely on hearing or sight.

## Not to be confused with

Red and green are not two sensory channels; both are visual. Adding shapes/labels can still reduce dependence on color discrimination. Multiple channels do not mean cues should be as loud or intrusive as possible.

## Use it for

A new objective comes with text, a symbol, and a sound; danger comes with audio, a directional marker, and vibration. Players need not rely solely on hearing or sight.

## Tunable parameters

visual cue · audio cue · haptic cue · semantic equivalence · channel controls · intensity

## Request prompt

Provide 3-channel feedback for a new objective: show text and a diamond icon for 4 seconds, play a 0.6-second sound, and vibrate for 150 ms; the event must remain understandable with any channel disabled, and red plus green does not count as 2 channels.

## Implementation hint

Publish one semantic event to visual, audio, and haptic presenters so each channel communicates the same core fact. Let players adjust or disable channels independently, and avoid multiplying intensity merely because several channels are active.
