# FX-17 · Layering

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

A heavy hit contains a sharp onset, a low body, and a fragment-like tail at the same time. Heard separately, they are different components; together, they form one event.

## Not to be confused with

Layering combines sounds simultaneously; random selection chooses one; concatenation plays them in sequence. These are different operations.

## Use it for

Assign contact, weight, material, and resonance to different components.

## Tunable parameters

onset layer · body layer · tail layer · sync offset · gain balance · frequency role · variation set

## Request prompt

Layer a hammer impact from three simultaneous parts: a 20–40 ms sharp onset, a roughly 180 ms low-frequency body, and a 0.8-second stone-fragment tail; keep start offsets within 10 ms and prevent masking in the 150–300 Hz range.

## Implementation hint

Build one event from simultaneous components with distinct roles, such as transient contact, low-frequency weight, and material tail. Balance timing and spectrum so the layers fuse without masking one another.
