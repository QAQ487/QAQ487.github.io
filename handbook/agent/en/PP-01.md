# PP-01 · Vignette

**Aliases:** Vignetting; Edge darkening; Lens vignette

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

The corners and edges of the image are darker (or tinted) than the centre, falling off smoothly from the middle outward, so the eye drifts to the centre.

## Not to be confused with

Not the low-health vignette (FB-16, a red, pulsing status cue driven by HP or hits); a plain vignette is a persistent, static lens style. Not exposure (REN-30, which changes overall brightness).

## Use it for

Cinematic feel, focusing on the hero, oppressive horror or flashback mood, hiding empty screen edges.

## Tunable parameters

intensity · inner radius · smoothness · roundness (aspect correction) · colour · centre offset

## Request prompt

Add a fullscreen vignette post-process to the game: circular centre (aspect-corrected to a true circle), darkening starts at 40% radius and reaches ×0.55 brightness in the corners; smoothstep falloff, default colour black #000000, intensity 0–1 (indoors 0.35, caves 0.6).

## Implementation hint

Fullscreen pass: d = length((uv-centre)*float2(aspect,1)); k = smoothstep(inner, outer, d); out.rgb = lerp(src.rgb, src.rgb*vignetteColour, k*intensity). Can be folded into the final tonemap/composite pass for free.
