# UX-23 · Wayfinding / Orientation / Navigation

**Aliases:** Critical path; Main path; Side path; Soft gate; 主路; 支路; 危險區提示

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

You recognize area features and realize you are back in the lobby, then use landmarks, maps, or signs to choose the next area. Orientation emphasizes locating yourself; navigation emphasizes the route.

## Not to be confused with

This does not mean controlling players with an arrow; players may know the main route and deliberately take a side path. It is also not the engine’s NPC pathfinding algorithm.

## Use it for

You recognize area features and realize you are back in the lobby, then use landmarks, maps, or signs to choose the next area. Orientation emphasizes locating yourself; navigation emphasizes the route. Landmarks, signs, or light should show which way continues toward the goal and which way is a branch the player can leave and rejoin. A riskier way can be marked without sealing it. (Former entries UX-31, “Main and side paths”, and UX-33, “Soft gate”, are merged here.)

## Tunable parameters

current location · destination · route choice · landmarks · map · confirmation

## Request prompt

Design navigation across 3 areas: players first identify the lobby by a large clock, then choose the laboratory or garden at 2 junctions; provide map and landmark confirmation at each junction, and show direction only after a 20 m deviation without forcing a return.

## Implementation hint

Support orientation with recognizable area identity and support navigation with route choices, landmarks, maps, or signs. Confirm progress at decision points without forcing players onto a single route or invoking NPC pathfinding concepts.
