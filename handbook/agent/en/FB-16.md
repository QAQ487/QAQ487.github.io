# FB-16 · Low-health vignette

**Aliases:** Damage vignette; Danger vignette; Red screen edges

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** UI feedback

## What you see

When health drops below a threshold, a red vignette appears around the screen edges and pulses like a heartbeat; it gets stronger and faster near death, sometimes desaturating the image.

## Not to be confused with

Not a one-off red flash of the whole screen when hit; the low-health vignette is a persistent state indicator.

## Use it for

Lets players feel "danger" without looking at the health bar; best paired with a heartbeat sound.

## Tunable parameters

threshold · max strength · inner radius · pulse bpm range · desaturation amount

## Request prompt

In your game, add a red vignette below 30% hero health: strength rises linearly to 0.6 at 0% health, pulsing as a double heartbeat from 70 to 110 bpm, and overall saturation drops by up to 30%.

## Implementation hint

Fullscreen post pass: vig = smoothstep(r0, r1, length(uv-0.5)*aspect); rgb = lerp(rgb, red, vig*strength*pulse). Run it after the low-res blit so the vignette is smooth, or before it for a pixelated look.
