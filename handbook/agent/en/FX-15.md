# FX-15 · Dissipation / Erosion

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Smoke or light fades, breaks apart, or has its edges gradually eaten away instead of disappearing abruptly.

## Not to be confused with

Reducing overall opacity is a fade; removing regions through a mask is erosion/dissolve. These can be combined. Decorative tails should not be mistaken for continuing damage.

## Use it for

Communicate the end of the energy and clear the image for the next event.

## Tunable parameters

fade duration · erosion threshold · edge noise · breakup scale · residual lifetime · damage cutoff

## Request prompt

After an energy column stops dealing damage, keep a 0.7-second exit: reduce overall opacity to 65% during the first 0.2 seconds, then erode from the edges to 100% with a noise mask over 0.5 seconds; disable damage at the start of the exit.

## Implementation hint

Animate opacity for broad fading and a spatial mask for local erosion, combining them when useful. End gameplay activity independently and make the remaining decorative tail visibly non-damaging.
