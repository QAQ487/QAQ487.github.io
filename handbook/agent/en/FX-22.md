# FX-22 · Spatialization / Panning

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

A sound source moves from left to right as you turn, or seems to be behind or above you.

## Not to be confused with

Left–right panning is not full 3D localization. HRTF/binaural processing differs from ordinary panning. Spatialization and distance attenuation are also separate dimensions.

## Use it for

Help players locate enemies, objects, and events.

## Tunable parameters

azimuth · elevation · spatial blend · listener orientation · HRTF mode · pan law · localization test

## Request prompt

Place an alarm 135° behind and to the listener’s right at a height of 2 m; as the player turns 360°, move the image continuously and test eight azimuths at three elevations on headphones while holding level constant to isolate localization.

## Implementation hint

Transform each source into listener-relative direction and render it with an appropriate panning or binaural method. Treat localization separately from distance attenuation and test front, rear, and elevation cues on supported outputs.
