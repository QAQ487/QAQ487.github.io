# FX-34 · Haptic Customization / Opt-out

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Haptics, Accessibility, and Cross-sensory Information

## What you see

Settings provide vibration toggles and strength controls. Essential gameplay information remains complete when vibration is off.

## Not to be confused with

Providing a slider does not establish safety. Percentages do not represent consistent physical output across devices, much less a medical safety measure.

## Use it for

Accommodate discomfort, pain, sensory load, and devices without vibration.

## Tunable parameters

master toggle · intensity steps · per-device scaling · default value · preview pattern · persistent setting · non-haptic fallback

## Request prompt

Add a master vibration toggle, a 0–100% intensity slider, and a one-second preview, defaulting to 50% with 5% steps and persistent storage; when disabled, retain every essential warning through visual and audio cues.

## Implementation hint

Expose a persistent master toggle and adjustable intensity, then scale requests through each device’s capability range. Ensure disabling haptics removes no gameplay information and do not describe percentage values as physical safety measurements.
