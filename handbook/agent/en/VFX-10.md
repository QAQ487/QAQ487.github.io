# VFX-10 · Lightning

**Aliases:** Electric arc; Lightning bolt; Chain lightning; Zap

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Combat VFX

## What you see

A jagged, branching bright line appears between two points, regenerating its shape every few frames, with a blue-white glow; chain lightning hops from one target to the next.

## Not to be confused with

Not a beam (VFX-11, a straight, stable, continuous line); an arc keeps jittering. Not a weather lightning flash (ENV-06, the whole screen lighting up).

## Use it for

Electric spells, tesla towers, chain attacks, machinery shorting out; the jitter itself says "dangerous, high-energy".

## Tunable parameters

subdivision depth · jitter amplitude (relative to length) · regen interval · branch probability · glow width · chain range & count

## Request prompt

Add a shock tower to the game: each attack fires an arc from the tower top to the nearest enemy, generated by midpoint displacement with 5 subdivision levels, re-randomised every 0.05 s for 0.25 s; then it chains from the first target to two more enemies within 60 px. White 1-px core with a pale-blue glow; struck enemies hit-flash.

## Implementation hint

CPU: recursive midpoint displacement (offset ∝ segment length × roughness), optional branches at random midpoints; upload as a line strip expanded to quads in a GS or on the CPU. Draw twice: wide low-alpha glow + thin white core, both additive. Seed the RNG with floor(time/regenInterval) for deterministic replay.
