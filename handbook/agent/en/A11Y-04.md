# A11Y-04 · Photosensitive flash suppression

**Aliases:** 閃光太刺眼; 爆炸白閃; 光敏模式; Photosensitivity mode; Flash reduction; Flash limiter; Epilepsy-safe mode; 光過敏対策

**Group:** Readability & accessibility
**Subcategory:** Visual modes & comfort

## What you see

Flashes in the game — explosion white-outs, hit flashes, lightning, flickering lights — pass through a central limiter: the number of large light–dark changes per second is capped, large-area flashes become slower fades or smaller regions, and saturated red flashes get special handling. Players can choose a Reduce flashing option in settings and see a photosensitivity warning at startup.

## Not to be confused with

Not the general option for less shaking and sliding (UX-20, covering shake, flashing, sliding and more) and not the low-health vignette (FB-16). This entry is specifically about limiting flash frequency and area through one rule that can also be checked automatically in builds.

## Use it for

Explosions, hit white-flashes, lightning, neon and glitch effects (PP-12), strobing lights in cutscenes.

## Tunable parameters

max large flashes per second ≤3 · large-area threshold ~25% of screen · fallback fade 0.2–0.5 s · strength scale 0.5 · red-flash rule · startup warning (thresholds after WCAG 2.3.1; verify per project)

## Request prompt

Route every flash through a central limiter: each flash requests through one function, and the limiter counts large-area (over 25% of the screen) light–dark changes in the last second; beyond 3, subsequent flashes become 0.3 s fades at half strength (the threshold follows WCAG's three-flashes rule; verify actual values for your project). Provide a Reduce flashing toggle that turns all full-screen white flashes into an edge-vignette cue.

## Implementation hint

FlashService.request(area, delta, colour): keep a 1 s ring buffer of accepted large transitions; if count>=limit or reduceFlashing: convert to fade (lerp over fallbackDur) and/or edge vignette; log violations in dev builds. Optional automated check: record frames, compute per-frame mean luminance over sliding windows, flag >3 paired transitions/s.
