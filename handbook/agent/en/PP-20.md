# PP-20 · False-colour infrared / Aerochrome

**Aliases:** False-colour infrared; Aerochrome; IR false colour; Wood effect; 偽色紅外攝影; 紅外假色; 紅葉紅外效果; 赤外線カラー写真; 疑似カラー赤外線; エアロクローム

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

Familiar landscapes become alien: vegetation turns crimson or pink against unusually dark or strongly contrasting skies. This is reflected near-infrared, not the heat view (PP-19). Include classic white-foliage “Wood effect” as a related infrared variant on the same card.

## Not to be confused with

REN-32 is general grading. PIX-06 swaps palette colours. This names a recognizable material-dependent spectral picture, not simply a global pink tint.

## Use it for

Alien landscapes, aerial looks, surreal travel.

## Tunable parameters

mode (Aerochrome / Wood B&W IR) · foliage map · sky darkening · painted-prop exclusion (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Enable infrared landscape treatment → foliage, sky and other surfaces → make foliage conspicuously red/pink (or white in Wood-effect mode) while keeping its response distinct from similarly green *painted* objects → restore visible-light appearance on disable.

## Implementation hint

Tag living foliage vs painted props. In Aerochrome mode remap foliage toward magenta/red and darken sky; painted green stays closer to visible green. Wood-effect mode: foliage → near-white, sky dark. Never a global pink multiply alone.
