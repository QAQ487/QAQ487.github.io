# UI-07 · Background blur on pause

**Aliases:** Pause blur; Backdrop blur; Frosted-glass background; Blurred backdrop

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** UI motion

## What you see

When the game is paused or a menu opens, the gameplay image behind it blurs and darkens slightly over ~0.2 s, the world freezes, and a sharp menu panel floats on top; closing it un-blurs and the world resumes.

## Not to be confused with

Not a fade to black (TRN-01, the screen is covered, used to change scenes); with a background blur the game is still faintly visible. Not depth of field (a lens effect blurring one depth range); the blur filter itself is PP-08.

## Use it for

Pause menus, inventory, settings, dialogue choices; focuses attention on the menu while the player still remembers where they are.

## Tunable parameters

blur radius · ramp time · dim colour & opacity · desaturation · snapshot vs live · panel entrance

## Request prompt

In your game, when the pause menu opens: freeze game time, blur a snapshot of the current frame to about an 8 px radius over 0.2 s with ease-out and overlay 35% dark blue #0d1630; slide the pause panel in from 12 px below while fading in; reverse over 0.15 s on close. Compute the blur once from a snapshot instead of re-rendering the scene every frame.

## Implementation hint

Copy the back buffer to a texture when pausing, then run a downsample chain (½, ¼, ⅛) with a small tent/Gaussian or dual-Kawase filter and upsample; lerp between blur levels for the ramp. Draw that texture fullscreen behind the menu. For pixel art, consider blurring at low resolution and upscaling with nearest-neighbour for a chunkier look.
