# PP-13 · Manga speed lines (focus lines)

**Aliases:** 漫畫速度線; 集中線; 衝刺線; Speed lines; Focus lines; Action lines; Anime speed lines

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

A ring of thin white or black wedges converges from the screen edges toward the centre, leaving the middle clear for the character; the lines redraw in place or rotate slightly. The viewer reads high-speed dashing, an impact, or a stretched moment of focus. A parallel horizontal version conveys fast sideways motion.

## Not to be confused with

Not radial blur (PP-07, which actually smears pixels outward) and not motion blur (REN-33, blur by object or camera velocity). Speed lines are an overlaid graphic symbol; the picture itself stays sharp.

## Use it for

Dashes, special-move charge and release, close-ups of surprise or resolve, stylised boost cues in racing.

## Tunable parameters

lines 40–80 · inner radius 35–50% H · width 1–4 px · refresh every 2–3 frames · opacity 0.5–0.8 · fade 0.1–0.2 s (starting points)

## Request prompt

Add a manga speed-line overlay: draw 40–80 thin triangular wedges around the screen centre (starting value), with inner ends at 35–50% of screen height from the centre and outer ends beyond the edge; re-randomise angle and length every 2–3 frames (about 8–12 updates per second, not a flash), opacity 0.5–0.8. Fade it in and out only during dashes or skills, and never cover the character at the centre.

## Implementation hint

Overlay pass or sprite batch: for i in lines: a = i/N*TAU + jitter(seed_i, frameStep); r0 = inner*(0.9+0.2*rand); draw triangle (center + dir(a)*r0) to two points at (center + dir(a±w)*R_outer). Reseed every k frames. Alternatively a polar-coordinate shader: mask = step(threshold, noise(angle*N, time_step)) * smoothstep(r0, r0+soft, r).
