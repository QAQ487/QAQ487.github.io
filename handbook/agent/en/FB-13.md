# FB-13 · Squash on hit

**Aliases:** Hit wobble; Hit squash and stretch; Jelly reaction

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

A struck object or character instantly squashes (wider and shorter) and bounces back, or wobbles side to side before settling — like jelly.

## Not to be confused with

Not the general squash-and-stretch of jumps (an animation principle) but a dedicated hit reaction; not a hit flash (FB-01, colour not shape).

## Use it for

Makes hits feel springy — for enemies, crates, bushes, even buttons; in pixel games a scale tween works without extra frames.

## Tunable parameters

squash scale · pivot (feet) · spring frequency & damping · tilt angle · pixel-snapping of the result

## Request prompt

In your game, squash enemies and barrels on hit around their feet: scale to (1.3, 0.75) within 0.04 s, then return to (1,1) with a damped spring over 0.25 s with one small overshoot; also tilt 8° along the knockback direction.

## Implementation hint

Per-instance scale/rotation in the sprite vertex shader (world = pivot + R*S*(local - pivot)). Point sampling keeps pixels crisp; optionally quantise scale to multiples of 1/spriteHeight to avoid shimmering.
