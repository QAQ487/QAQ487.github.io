# UX-01 · HUD

**Aliases:** Heads-up display

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** On-screen Information and Menus

## What you see

Health, ammunition, a compass, and similar information stay on screen while you move or fight, showing your current status without opening a menu. Search `game HUD design`.

## Not to be confused with

The HUD is not the entire UI; inventory and settings screens are UI too. A HUD need not remain visible at all times: it can fade out according to context.

## Use it for

Health, ammunition, a compass, and similar information stay on screen while you move or fight, showing your current status without opening a menu. Search `game HUD design`.

## Tunable parameters

health · ammunition · compass · visibility rules · safe margins · combat state

## Request prompt

Design a 16:9 combat HUD with 0–100 health at bottom left, 30/120 ammunition at bottom right, and an 8-point compass at the top; fade ammunition 5 seconds after combat, but keep health visible below 25%.

## Implementation hint

Bind each HUD element to live player state and a small set of visibility states such as exploration, combat, and menus. Keep critical status readable within safe margins, and fade only information that is not currently needed.
