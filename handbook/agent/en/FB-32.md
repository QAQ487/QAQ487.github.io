# FB-32 · Projectile reflect / Batting

**Aliases:** Projectile reflect; Deflect projectile; Bat back; Return fire with melee; Projectile parry; 拍返飛行道具; 彈返子彈; 反彈光彈; 弾き返し; 反射打ち

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

An enemy bullet, energy orb or arrow flies at the player; the player swings so the active hitbox meets it: sparks burst at the contact point with a brief pause, then the projectile turns around and flies back faster than it came, its colour switching from the enemy's (e.g. red) to the player's (e.g. cyan) with a longer, brighter trail. It now hurts only enemies, and hitting the original shooter plays an extra-large impact. Swinging too early or too late lets the projectile hit the player as usual.

## Not to be confused with

Not a perfect parry (FB-26, the opponent's melee weapon is knocked away; nothing flies back). Not shield absorption (MATX-08, the projectile vanishes on the shield). Not telekinetic grab and throw (FB-33, the object is caught, held floating in front of the player and then aimed). Batting sends the projectile back within a single swing.

## Use it for

Melee weapons deflecting energy bolts, volleying an energy ball back and forth with a boss, melee-and-gun hybrids, teaching players to answer ranged attacks with melee.

## Tunable parameters

reflect window = attack active frames · hitstop 3–5 f · return speed ×1.3–1.8 · aim: back to shooter (assist cone) vs swing direction · team colour swap · trail length boost · reflectable vs non-reflectable look (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add projectile batting to melee. While an attack hitbox is active and overlaps a reflectable enemy projectile: hitstop 3–5 frames, sparks and a crisp impact sound at the contact; switch the projectile to the player's team and recolour it from enemy red to player cyan; aim it at the original shooter if within an aim-assist cone, otherwise along the swing direction; multiply its speed by 1.3–1.8 and lengthen the trail. Hitting the original shooter plays an enlarged impact. Give non-reflectable projectiles a different shape and colour (see NAV-16). Accept: early, on-time and late swings give three clearly different results, and a batted projectile never hurts the player.

## Implementation hint

Projectiles carry team, velocity and a reflectable flag. When an active melee hitbox overlaps a reflectable enemy projectile, flip its team, swap its material or palette, set its direction toward the original shooter if inside an aim-assist cone (else along the swing direction), multiply speed, extend the trail, reset its lifetime and apply a short hitstop to the batter. Ignore further overlaps with the player who reflected it.
