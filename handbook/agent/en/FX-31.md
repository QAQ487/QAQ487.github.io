# FX-31 · Stinger

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Picking up an important item or triggering an event adds a short musical cue over the current score without necessarily replacing the entire background track.

## Not to be confused with

An ordinary UI beep is not necessarily a musical stinger. Stingers can be scheduled to align with beats, rather than always playing unconditionally the instant an event occurs.

## Use it for

Give rewards, discoveries, and turning points a musical marker.

## Tunable parameters

cue length · beat quantization · overlay gain · cooldown · event priority · tonal variant · interruption rule

## Request prompt

When a rare item is collected, overlay a 1.5-second musical stinger on the continuing score; if the next beat is within 250 ms, wait for it, otherwise play immediately, with a 3-second same-type cooldown and a peak 3 dB above the score.

## Implementation hint

Overlay a short musical phrase on the continuing score and schedule it at an appropriate beat or bar when latency permits. Define priority and cooldown rules so frequent events do not create musical clutter.
