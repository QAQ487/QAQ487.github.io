# UX-27 · Sightline / Line of sight（LOS）

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

Seeing the next area’s exit through a doorway or an enemy at the end of a corridor provides information for observation, planning, or combat.

## Not to be confused with

A sightline does not guarantee that players look, much less remember what they saw. Distinguish combat LOS, glass that allows sight but blocks shots, and navigational sightlines according to the game’s rules.

## Use it for

Seeing the next area’s exit through a doorway or an enemy at the end of a corridor provides information for observation, planning, or combat.

## Tunable parameters

observer point · target point · occlusion · glass · shot blocking · route visibility

## Request prompt

Create a 25 m sightline from the corridor entrance to the next exit and show 1 enemy through glass; the glass permits sight but blocks movement and shots, while the actual entrance is 12 m to the right, so visibility must not imply direct access.

## Implementation hint

Evaluate visual connection separately from movement and projectile rules. Frame useful targets from likely observer positions, then record whether each intervening material blocks sight, traversal, or shots independently.
