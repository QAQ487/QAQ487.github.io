# FB-36 · Percentage knockback & trajectory influence

**Aliases:** Percentage knockback; Damage percentage; Launch scaling; Knockback scaling; Directional influence; DI; Launch trail; Smoke trail; 百分比擊飛; 累積傷害擊飛; 軌道影響; 擊飛軌跡; ふっとび率; ベクトル変更; ふっとびの煙

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

There is no health bar: under each fighter is just an accumulated-damage percentage, and as it climbs the number shifts from white to red to dark red, jumping a little on every hit. At a low percentage a move only knocks the opponent back a short way; at a high percentage the hit lands with a brief freeze, then the opponent is sent flying a long way with a trail of smoke behind them that draws the flight path. During that freeze the victim can push the stick to bend the flight direction slightly, and the smoke trail curves off to one side with it, giving them a chance not to fly off the stage. Crossing the stage boundary sets off a coloured burst at the edge and the number resets to zero.

## Not to be confused with

Not ordinary knockback (ACT-29, a fixed push distance that does not change with accumulated damage). Not hitstop itself (ACT-21, only the freeze at the moment of contact). Not a health bar: the percentage never runs down to zero, it only accumulates. This card holds no formula, only what is seen.

## Use it for

Platform fighters, party games won by knocking players off the stage, and any versus game that wants 'the more you are hit, the farther you fly' to show building pressure.

## Tunable parameters

damage % readout & colour ramp · number bump on hit · launch distance grows with % (the curve is left to the game) · hitstop grows with launch strength · smoke trail only on strong launches · trajectory steer during hitstop (small, capped) · boundary burst & reset (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add a percentage-knockback presentation for fighters. Replace the health bar with an accumulated-damage percentage that shifts from white to red to dark red as it rises and jumps on every hit. Make launch distance grow with the percentage; give high-percentage hits a more noticeable hitstop and leave a smoke trail behind the launched fighter. During hitstop, read the victim's stick and allow it to bend the launch direction by only a small amount, with the trail showing the bent direction. When a fighter crosses the stage boundary, play a knock-out burst at that edge and reset the number. Accept: the same move landed at a low, middle and high percentage gives three clearly different flight distances on screen, and the trails with and without stick input are visibly different.

## Implementation hint

Keep an accumulated damage value per fighter and show it as a percentage with a colour ramp. On hit, take launch strength from the move's data and the target's current value through a designer-tuned curve, set hitstop from that strength, and during hitstop read the victim's stick to rotate the launch angle by a small capped amount (input perpendicular to the launch steers most). After hitstop, apply the launch velocity, start a trail emitter for launches above a threshold, and play a burst where the fighter crosses the stage bounds.
