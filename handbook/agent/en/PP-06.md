# PP-06 · Desaturation

**Aliases:** Grayscale; Monochrome; Black and white filter; Saturation reduction

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

The image’s colours drain until only black, white and grey remain; brightness relationships stay the same, only colour disappears. It can also remove only part of the saturation, or keep one colour.

## Not to be confused with

Not colour grading (REN-32, which shifts tones rather than simply removing colour) nor a status tint (FB-05, adding a colour rather than removing it). Grayscale reads as same brightness, no colour.

## Use it for

Death or game-over screens, flashbacks, pause or time-stop, highlighting the one object that keeps its colour (selective colour).

## Tunable parameters

saturation amount · luminance weights · ramp time · keep-hue range · tint after desaturation

## Request prompt

Add a desaturation post-process to the game: luminance with Rec.709 weights L=0.2126R+0.7152G+0.0722B, output lerp(colour, L, s); on player death ramp s from 0 to 1 over 0.8 s; flashback scenes hold s=0.7 with a slight warm multiply #f0e0c0; optionally keep reds (pixels within ±20° hue are not desaturated).

## Implementation hint

Fullscreen pass: L = dot(rgb, float3(0.2126,0.7152,0.0722)) (on linear colour, or the gamma-space approximation); out = lerp(rgb, L.xxx, s). Selective colour: compute hue, s *= 1 - smoothstep(range, range+feather, hueDistance).
