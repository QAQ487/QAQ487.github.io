# FX-12 · Value / Saturation Hierarchy

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

The focal point of an important ability stands out from its supporting elements. Light and dark remain grouped in grayscale, rather than depending only on red-versus-blue distinctions.

## Not to be confused with

Value is not hue. High saturation also does not guarantee sufficient contrast against the background. LoL’s art rules should not be copied wholesale as standards for every game.

## Use it for

Guide attention and communicate an event’s importance.

## Tunable parameters

focal value · support value · saturation range · background contrast · grayscale groups · peak area

## Request prompt

Reserve the brightest values for the central 10% of the ability, maintain at least a 30% value difference between the main form and background, and reduce supporting saturation to roughly half the core; verify the focal point in grayscale over three representative scenes.

## Implementation hint

Assign distinct value and saturation bands to the focal event, supporting forms, and background. Review in grayscale and against representative scenes because saturation alone cannot guarantee separation.
