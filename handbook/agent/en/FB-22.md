# FB-22 · Weapon weight: sway and inertia lag

**Aliases:** 武器太輕; 槍像貼在畫面上; 武器晃動; Weapon sway; Weapon inertia; View-model lag; Weapon bob; ウェポンスウェイ

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Aiming & handling

## What you see

When the first-person or over-the-shoulder camera turns, the held weapon is not glued to the screen: it lags slightly and catches up to centre; while walking it bobs with the steps and slowly recentres on stopping. Heavy weapons lag more and recentre more slowly, light weapons respond faster. The lag is purely visual — the reticle and fire direction are unaffected.

## Not to be confused with

Not recoil camera kick (FB-08, camera movement on firing) and not reticle bloom (FB-19, the spread area). This entry is only the held model's lag and sway relative to the camera.

## Use it for

First-person shooters, held models for melee weapons, hand-held lamps or tools, equipment systems that must convey weapon weight.

## Tunable parameters

spring stiffness 40–150 · damping ratio 0.6–0.9 · max offset 2–5° · walk bob 0.5–2 cm · reduced-motion scale 0.3 (starting points)

## Request prompt

Add weight to the first-person weapon model: when the camera rotates, the model follows the camera direction with a damped spring (stiffness 120 for light weapons, 60 for heavy, damping ratio 0.8 — all starting values), with a maximum offset of 3°; add 1.5 cm of step bob while walking. The reticle and bullet direction always come from the camera centre, and the reduced-motion setting scales the sway to 30%.

## Implementation hint

Keep a lagged orientation q_w; each frame: angular error e = camRot ⊖ q_w; angVel += (k*e - c*angVel)*dt; q_w ⊕= angVel*dt; clamp |e| to maxOffset. Render the view model at q_w plus bob offset = (sin(phase)*a, |sin(phase)|*b). Fire rays from the camera, not the model.
