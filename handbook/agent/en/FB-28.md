# FB-28 · Unblockable attack signifier

**Aliases:** Unblockable warning; Perilous attack symbol; Danger kanji; Red unblockable glow; Guard-break warning; 不可防禦警示; 危字提示; ガード不能の予兆; 危険攻撃; 危

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Combat effect feedback

## What you see

When an enemy starts an attack that cannot be blocked, a distinctive symbol pops above or in front of it (e.g. a red danger kanji or a red flare) together with a unique warning sound. The symbol is drawn on top even when the enemy is behind a pillar or other enemies, so it reads from far away; a small sub-icon tells which answer to use (jump, dodge, or a dedicated counter). It disappears when the attack fires or is interrupted.

## Not to be confused with

Not a general attack telegraph (NAV-04 — ground or body cues for attack area or direction); this card only says this move cannot be blocked and which category of answer works. Not the threat direction ring (UI-27 — direction of off-screen attackers). Not wind-up VFX (FX-13 — the charge glow every attack shares).

## Use it for

Guard-centric action combat where certain moves must be answered with a dodge, jump or special counter instead.

## Tunable parameters

symbol shape & colour (one per game) · pop-in scale 0.1 s · lead time before active frames · draw-on-top / occlusion bypass · cue sound · sub-icon per answer category (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add an unblockable warning to enemy attacks. At wind-up start, show a red warning symbol above the enemy's head with a unique cue sound; the symbol pops in with a scale-up within 0.1 s and stays until the active frames start. Always draw it on top so scenery and other enemies never hide it. Add a small sub-icon per answer category (jump, dodge, special counter) while the main symbol keeps one shape and colour. Remove it at once if the attack is interrupted. Accept: an enemy attacking from behind a pillar still shows the symbol, and the three answer categories are distinguishable at a glance.

## Implementation hint

Tag attacks with unblockable + answerCategory. On the wind-up anim event, spawn a world-anchored billboard at the head socket rendered in an overlay pass with depth test off, play the cue sound, and keep it until the active-frame event or an interrupt event. Choose the sub-icon from answerCategory. Reserve the symbol colour for this cue only.
