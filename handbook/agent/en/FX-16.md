# FX-16 · Gameplay Clarity / Visual Noise Budget

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Characters, ground boundaries, and the most important abilities remain visible in a crowded fight. Not every small effect competes to be the brightest point on screen.

## Not to be confused with

Clarity does not mean eliminating effects. Important abilities can be striking; the question is whether a hierarchy remains. A “low effects” setting should not also remove danger cues.

## Use it for

Allocate attention to events that actually require a response.

## Tunable parameters

priority tier · particle budget · screen coverage · danger boundary · character visibility · low-effects floor · crowd test

## Request prompt

Test clarity in a stress scene with eight characters and 12 simultaneous abilities: cap decorative particles at 1,200, limit each secondary effect to no more than 8% screen coverage, and preserve every hazard boundary in low-effects mode.

## Implementation hint

Assign effects to priority tiers and cap decorative density or coverage before reducing decision-critical cues. Test representative crowded scenes, ensuring low-effects modes preserve characters, targeting information, and hazard boundaries.
