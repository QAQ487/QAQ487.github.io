# FB-10 · Slow motion

**Aliases:** Bullet time; Time dilation; Slow-mo

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Time manipulation

## What you see

Game time slows down globally (e.g. to 30%), so characters, particles and projectiles all move slowly; sometimes the player stays faster, and the screen edges darken or desaturate.

## Not to be confused with

Not a freeze or hitstop (fully stopped) nor a freeze-frame finisher (FB-11). Slow-mo still moves, just slower, and can last seconds.

## Use it for

Abilities (bullet time), perfect-dodge rewards, drama when the last enemy falls.

## Tunable parameters

time scale 0.1–0.5 · ramp in/out · duration · player exemption factor · post effect (desaturate, vignette) · audio pitch

## Request prompt

In your game, trigger 1.5 s of slow motion after a perfect dodge: drop global timeScale to 0.3 within 0.1 s and ramp back to 1 over the final 0.3 s; meanwhile desaturate 40% with a pale-blue vignette, and keep the hero animating at 0.6×.

## Implementation hint

Two clocks: gameDelta = realDelta * timeScale; UI and post-process use realDelta. Post: fullscreen pass with saturation lerp and vignette in a pixel shader. Particles must use gameDelta to slow correctly.
