# UI-15 · Typewriter effect

**Aliases:** Text crawl; Letter-by-letter reveal; Character reveal; Scrolling text

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** Text & dialogue

## What you see

Dialogue or instructions appear one character at a time, like a typewriter; it pauses briefly at punctuation, and when the line is complete a bobbing "▼" appears in the corner to show you can continue; pressing a key while text is still appearing reveals the whole line at once.

## Not to be confused with

Not animated text (UI-17, already-visible text that waves or jitters); a typewriter only controls when characters appear. Not the dialogue box itself (UI-16, the frame and name tag that hold the text).

## Use it for

Dialogue, tutorials, narration; controls reading pace and conveys tone (slow = hesitant, fast = excited), often paired with blip voice sounds.

## Tunable parameters

characters per second · punctuation pauses · blip sound interval & pitch · skip behaviour (finish line / fast-forward) · continue indicator

## Request prompt

Add a typewriter effect to your game’s dialogue: 30 characters per second, a 0.15 s pause after commas and 0.35 s after full stops, question and exclamation marks; play a short voice blip every 2 characters; lay out the whole line first and then reveal it, so words never jump to the next line mid-reveal; pressing confirm while typing shows the full line instantly, and once complete show a "▼" in the bottom-right that bobs 2 px on a 0.6 s cycle.

## Implementation hint

Lay out the full string once (line breaks, glyph positions), then each frame set visibleCount = number of glyphs whose scheduled reveal time ≤ elapsed; schedule = cumulative sum of 1/cps plus punctuation pauses. Rich-text tags must be excluded from the count. Advance on input: if visibleCount < total, reveal all; else go to the next line.
