# UI-05 · Cooldown sweep / Radial fill

**Aliases:** Cooldown ring; Radial cooldown; Clock-wipe cooldown; Radial fill

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** UI motion

## What you see

After a skill is used its icon is covered by a translucent dark layer that sweeps away clockwise (or anticlockwise) like a clock hand, often with the remaining seconds in the middle; when the cooldown ends the icon flashes white or its rim glows to say "ready".

## Not to be confused with

Not a clock-wipe transition (TRN-05, the same radial mask used to change the whole screen); a cooldown sweep sits on one icon and shows time. Not an ordinary horizontal progress bar.

## Use it for

Skill bars, item cooldowns, build/production timers, hold-to-interact progress. On small icons a radial sweep reads faster than a bar.

## Tunable parameters

start angle · direction · mask colour & opacity · number format · ready flash & ring · shared vs per-skill timers

## Request prompt

Add cooldown sweeps to your game’s skill bar: after use, cover the 24×24 icon with a 60% black wedge that sweeps open clockwise from 12 o’clock over the cooldown duration; show whole seconds in the centre while more than 1 s remains and one decimal below 1 s; when ready, flash the icon white for 0.1 s and expand a 2 px white ring outward over 0.25 s.

## Implementation hint

In the icon pixel shader compute a = atan2(uv - 0.5) remapped to [0,1) from 12 o’clock; darken where a > progress. Or build a triangle-fan wedge mesh on the CPU. Drive progress from game time (pauses with gameplay) and keep the number on a separate text layer.
