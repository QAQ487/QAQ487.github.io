# PP-14 · Bleach bypass / Skip bleach

**Aliases:** Bleach bypass; Skip bleach; Silver retention; 留銀效果; 漂白旁路; 銀残し; ブリーチバイパス

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

A hard, gritty image with heavy blacks, reduced colour, and forceful highlight contrast. Some colour remains — it is not a black-and-white convert. A blue/cyan cast is optional, not defining.

## Not to be confused with

REN-32 is grading in general; PP-06 removes saturation; REN-31 shapes tonal response. This names a recognizable combination, not simply “less colour.”

## Use it for

War, grounded gunplay, scenes that need a gritty film look.

## Tunable parameters

saturation retention · black crush · contrast · optional cyan cast · HUD exclude (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Select gritty presentation → scene image, excluding HUD → deepen blacks, reduce saturation and harden contrast while retaining some colour → restore the clean picture when disabled.

## Implementation hint

Fullscreen grade after tonemap: desaturate partially, lift contrast (S-curve), crush lows, optionally add a cool cast to mids. Keep UI/HUD on an ungraded layer.
