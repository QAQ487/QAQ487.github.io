# FB-06 · Critical hit emphasis

**Aliases:** Crit feedback; Critical hit effect; Crit pop

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

On a crit the normal hit feedback is amplified across the board: bigger, differently coloured damage number, longer hitstop, stronger shake, an extra star spark or flash, sometimes a brief slow-down.

## Not to be confused with

Not a separate effect but FB-01/02/07 "turned up"; unlike a freeze-frame finisher (FB-11), a crit does not end the fight.

## Use it for

Makes random or skill-based big hits feel rewarding, so players can tell a normal hit from a crit.

## Tunable parameters

number scale & colour · hitstop multiplier · extra shake trauma · spark shape · optional time-scale dip

## Request prompt

In your game, make crits clearly different from normal hits: gold damage number at 1.6× scale with "!"; hitstop 0.05 s → 0.1 s; add +0.4 shake trauma; spawn a white 4-point star spark at the impact point for 0.15 s.

## Implementation hint

No new rendering path: a CritEvent multiplies parameters of existing feedback systems (hitstop, shake, numbers, spark emitter). Keep the multipliers in one data table so design can tune them.
