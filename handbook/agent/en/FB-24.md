# FB-24 · Damage vibration

**Aliases:** Hitstop shake; Hitlag vibration; Victim shake; Damage shake; 受擊顫動; ダメージバイブ

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

On contact, attacker/victim roots buzz with a rapidly alternating, decaying local offset while the world can stay stable — force buzzing through the body, unlike a fully static pause.

## Not to be confused with

Not camera shake (CAM-30 / FB-07), not hitstop clocks alone (ACT-21), and not squash-on-hit (FB-13). Vibration is a render-only local offset that must reach exactly zero when hitstop ends.

## Use it for

Fighters, action games, contacts that need electric weight.

## Tunable parameters

freq · victim/attacker amp · decay · grounded horizontal-only · clamp screen px · reduced-motion flag (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

On confirmed contact, during hitstop apply a rapidly alternating decaying local offset to attacker/victim roots (victim stronger; grounded: horizontal only). Hurtboxes stay fixed. Offset is exactly 0 when hitstop ends. Reduced motion may disable vibration while keeping hitstop.

## Implementation hint

While hitstop active: renderRoot = gameplayRoot + alternate(±amp)*exp(-k*t) on chosen axes. Never write the offset into physics/hurtboxes. On hitstop end force offset=0 to avoid a pop. Demo FREEZE vs VIBRATE lanes with a stable background.
