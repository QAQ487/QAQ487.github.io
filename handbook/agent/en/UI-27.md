# UI-27 · Threat direction ring

**Aliases:** Threat indicator; Threat ring; Incoming attack indicator; Off-screen attack warning; Attack direction arc; 威脅方向環; 背後攻擊預警; 脅威インジケーター; 被攻撃方向リング

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** HUD & interface types

## What you see

A faint ring sits under the player character. When an enemy outside the view (behind or behind-left/right) starts an attack, the segment of the ring facing that enemy lights up as a short arc. Arc colour encodes the threat type (e.g. red for melee, purple for ranged). As the hit approaches, the arc thickens, brightens and pulses faster. It fades out immediately when the player dodges, parries or gets hit, and disappears if the enemy cancels the attack.

## Not to be confused with

Not an off-screen edge arrow (UI-09 — points at a target or enemy position from the screen edge, says nothing about attack timing). Not an attack telegraph (NAV-04 — marks the area about to be hit on the ground or on a visible enemy). This ring hugs the player and lights only while an unseen enemy is actually winding up. Not a damage-direction indicator shown after you are hit.

## Use it for

Third-person melee, crowds surrounding the player, over-the-shoulder cameras that cannot see behind; tells the player which way to dodge without turning the camera.

## Tunable parameters

ring radius & height · arc width (deg) · colour per threat type · thicken / pulse curve toward impact · on-screen suppression · fade-out 0.1–0.2 s · max simultaneous arcs (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add a threat direction ring to third-person combat. When an off-screen enemy starts an attack, light a short arc on a ring under the player in that enemy's direction, coloured by threat type (melee / ranged / unblockable). From wind-up to impact the arc thickens, brightens and pulses faster. Fade it within 0.1–0.2 s when the player dodges, parries or is hit, or when the enemy cancels; enemies visible on screen get no arc. Accept: two enemies behind and behind-right attacking at once produce two correctly aimed arcs with independent timing.

## Implementation hint

For each enemy in wind-up whose position is outside the view (or behind the camera), compute its bearing relative to the player in camera-yaw space and draw an arc segment on a ground-projected ring (or a screen-space ring around the player anchor). Drive thickness, brightness and pulse rate from normalized time-to-impact taken from the attack's wind-up and active anim events. Pick colour from an attack-type tag. Remove the arc on dodge / parry / hit / cancel events and merge arcs closer than a minimum angle.
