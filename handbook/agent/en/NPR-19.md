# NPR-19 · Papercraft / paper-world construction

**Aliases:** Papercraft; Paper theatre; Construction-paper world; Layered paper diorama; 剪紙; 紙藝世界; 層疊色紙; ペーパークラフト; 切り紙; 紙の世界

**Group:** Stylized rendering (NPR)
**Subcategory:** Retro & lo-fi

## What you see

The world reads as cut, stacked, or folded paper: crisp silhouettes, visible thickness or stacked planes, paper fibre, and shadows between layers. It is a construction, not a paint filter.

## Not to be confused with

Not HD-2D (NPR-14). Not faceted low-poly (NPR-12). Not a generic drop shadow alone (LGT-10).

## Use it for

Paper-theatre titles, children’s-book worlds, handmade cutaways.

## Tunable parameters

layer count · edge thickness · inter-layer shadow · paper texture · hinge motion (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Enable papercraft presentation → scene elements → each reads as a cut paper layer with a thin edge, slight offset shadow, and paper texture; motion can be as hinged or sliding layers → restore ordinary solids on disable.

## Implementation hint

Render elements as offset flat cards with extruded thin edges and contact shadows between layers; sample a paper-fibre texture. Animate as sliding/hinged planes rather than skinned meshes.
