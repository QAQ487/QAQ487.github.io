# FB-30 · Weapon recall / Return-path pierce

**Aliases:** Weapon recall; Axe recall; Recall throw; Return pierce; Thrown weapon return; 召回武器; 回收飛斧; 武器回収; 呼び戻し; 投擲武器の帰還

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

A thrown weapon is stuck in a wall or an enemy. On recall it first shudders and is tugged free, then flies back to the character's hand along a slightly curved arc, speeding up as it comes; enemies on the return path are hit or dragged toward the character. The weapon lands in the hand with a heavy smack, the arm is pushed back a little by the momentum, and the camera gives a small kick.

## Not to be confused with

Not a boomerang (a fixed out-and-back path tied to the throw). Here the weapon flies from where it is now to where the hand is now, and can hit things on the way. Not a projectile trail (VFX-08 — the trail itself). Not pickup magnetism (FB-14 — drops pulled toward the player).

## Use it for

Throwable signature weapons, tethered weapons, play where pulling the weapon back is also an attack.

## Tunable parameters

dislodge shake 0.1–0.2 s · curve bend · acceleration profile · catch recoil pose · camera kick on catch · return-path hit radius · pull vs hit (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add recall to a throwable weapon. On recall, shake the weapon for 0.1–0.2 s and pull it free, then fly it back along an arc that bends toward the character, accelerating from slow to fast and re-aiming every frame from its current position to the character's current hand. Enemies crossed on the way are hit or pulled toward the character. On arrival play a catch: arm pushed back, light camera kick, heavy smack. Accept: recalling while the character moves still lands in the hand, and two enemies on the return line both get hit.

## Implementation hint

Store the stuck transform. On recall play a short shake and dislodge offset, then move along a quadratic / cubic curve from the current weapon position to the live hand socket, re-evaluated every frame, with speed rising on an ease-in curve. Sweep a sphere along each frame's segment to collect hits (each target once). On arriving inside the catch radius, attach to the hand and fire the catch anim event (recoil, camera kick, sound).
