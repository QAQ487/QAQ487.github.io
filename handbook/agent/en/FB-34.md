# FB-34 · Inhale & spit

**Aliases:** Inhale and spit; Inhale; Suction; Vacuum inhale; Swallow and spit; Star spit; Vacuum gun; Tongue swallow; 吸入與吐出; 吸入; 吸塵吸入; 吞吐; 吸い込み; 吐き出し; バキューム

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

The character opens its mouth wide (or raises the nozzle of a vacuum), and a cone of suction appears in front of it, drawn as wind lines flowing inward. Small enemies and objects inside are dragged toward the character, their bodies stretched and their feet scraping the ground, faster the closer they get; enemies that are too big only sway and resist. Once a target is sucked into the mouth, the character's body puffs up with its cheeks full. On attack the character spits it out as a star or pellet that flies straight ahead and shatters on an enemy or a wall; on down it swallows it instead and the bulge goes away. Variant — vacuum: a captured target struggles and pulls away, and the player has to pull the stick the other way to reel it in; whatever has been sucked up can also be blasted back out of the nozzle.

## Not to be confused with

Not pickup magnetism (FB-14, where drops fly to the player with no action needed). Not telekinesis (FB-33, where the object is held in front and then thrown). Not possession (ACT-53). Gaining an ability by swallowing is the copy ability (FB-35).

## Use it for

A basic attack in platformers, turning enemies into ammunition, sucking up objects and dust from the floor, catching ghosts or small creatures.

## Tunable parameters

cone length & angle · pull acceleration & stretch amount · weight classes (pullable / resists) · inward wind lines · bloated look · spit projectile speed & break on hit · swallow input · tug-of-war variant for heavy targets (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add inhale and spit. While the inhale button is held, create a cone of suction in front and show wind lines flowing inward; light targets inside are pulled toward the character, faster as they get closer, and stretched along the pull; heavy targets only sway. When a target reaches the mouth, remove it and put the character in a bloated state. While bloated, attack spits out a star-shaped projectile that flies straight and shatters on hit, damaging what it hits; down swallows it and ends the bloat. Accept: inhaling two targets at once still yields only one projectile, and heavy targets are never sucked in.

## Implementation hint

While inhaling, query targets inside the cone; for each pullable one, apply a pull toward the mouth that grows as the distance shrinks, override its AI with a struggle animation and scale it along the pull direction. On reaching the mouth, despawn it and store its type in a single mouth slot (one projectile however many were inhaled). Spit spawns a straight projectile that breaks on contact; swallow clears the slot and can pass the stored type to the copy-ability system (FB-35).
