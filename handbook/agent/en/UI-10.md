# UI-10 · Floating nameplate / Overhead bar

**Aliases:** Nameplate; Overhead health bar; Unit frame above head; World-space health bar

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** HUD & interface types

## What you see

A name and a thin health bar float above a character or enemy and follow it; the name colour shows allegiance (red enemy, green ally), the bar often appears or brightens only after damage and fades after a while without being hit; text size does not change with camera distance.

## Not to be confused with

Not the fixed HUD health bar (the player’s own resource meter); an overhead bar travels with the unit. Not damage numbers (FB-02, brief pop-ups per hit). Not the delayed health bar (FB-17, which is a drain animation you can apply to an overhead bar).

## Use it for

Action RPGs, tower defence, RTS, multiplayer; reading many units’ states without clicking each one.

## Tunable parameters

size & offset above head · show rule (always / damaged / targeted) · fade delay · allegiance colours · overlap handling · max distance

## Request prompt

Add overhead health bars for enemies in your game: 16 px wide, 2 px tall, 4 px above the sprite’s head, red #e04a3a with a 1 px dark outline; show it only after the enemy is damaged and fade it out over 0.3 s after 3 s without damage; draw the name in a 6 px pixel font above the bar, red for enemies and green for allies; keep bar and name on integer pixel coordinates, never rotated or scaled by the camera.

## Implementation hint

Each frame project the unit’s head anchor to screen space, snap to whole pixels and draw the plate in the UI pass (screen-space size). Batch all plates into one dynamic vertex buffer. Hide or stack plates that overlap; cull by distance and when the unit is off-screen.
