# FX-27 · Reverb / Dry–Wet Balance

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

A clap is followed by a dense, spreading spatial tail. Nearby direct sound is clearer; the proportion of reverb may increase farther away.

## Not to be confused with

Distinct repeated sounds are more characteristic of echo/delay; reverb usually forms a blended reflection tail. Adding reverb does not mean real geometry or diffraction has been calculated.

## Use it for

Convey a sense of space and place the source in its environment.

## Tunable parameters

dry level · wet level · decay time · pre-delay · room profile · distance send · transition time

## Request prompt

For a clap in a stone chamber, use 25 ms pre-delay and a 1.8-second reverb tail; set the dry–wet balance to 80:20 at 2 m and 45:55 at 15 m, with a 0.6-second crossfade between rooms.

## Implementation hint

Send sources to a room-appropriate reverberant path while preserving a controllable dry signal. Blend room changes and dry–wet ratios smoothly, and keep dense reverberation distinct from discrete delay repeats.
