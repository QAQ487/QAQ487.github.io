# PP-07 · Radial blur

**Aliases:** Zoom blur; Zoom burst; Radial motion blur; Speed blur

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Blur & distortion

## What you see

Blur streaks radiate outward from a centre point; the centre stays sharp and the outskirts smear more, like a lens zooming in fast.

## Not to be confused with

Not motion blur (REN-33, blur along the real velocity of objects or the camera); radial blur always streaks out from one point. Not a whip pan (TRN-14, a fast horizontal sweep).

## Use it for

Dashes/boosts, charge-up or super-move moments, heavy impacts, teleporting or entering another realm.

## Tunable parameters

centre · strength (streak length) · sample count · clear radius · ramp in/out

## Request prompt

Add a radial-blur post-process to the game: centre on the player’s screen position, take 10 samples along the pixel→centre direction over 12% of the distance (strength 0.12), no blur inside 15% radius; on a dash ramp strength to 0.12 over 0.1 s, then ease out to 0 over 0.3 s.

## Implementation hint

Fullscreen pass: dir = centre - uv; accumulate N samples of src at uv + dir*strength*(i/N), weighted equally (or falling off); mask with smoothstep(clearRadius, clearRadius+0.1, length(dir)). Two cheaper passes of N/2 samples with the second at half step approximates more samples.
