# FB-23 · Weapon pull-back near obstacles

**Aliases:** Weapon wall collision; Gun tuck; Weapon lowering near wall; Barrel retract; 槍枝收槍; 壁際の銃退避

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Aiming & handling

## What you see

Standing against a wall with a gun, the character tucks or raises the weapon instead of letting the barrel clip through.

## Not to be confused with

Not weapon sway/inertia (FB-22) and not camera recoil kick (FB-08). This is collision-driven pose.

## Use it for

Realistic FPS/TPS, close-quarters gunplay.

## Tunable parameters

trace length · retract/raise pose · blend speed · max weight · third-person socket (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

When a muzzle-forward trace hits closer than weapon length, blend in a retract/raise pose proportional to penetration; blend back when the trace is clear.

## Implementation hint

Raycast from shoulder/muzzle along aim. weight = saturate(1 - hitDist/weaponLength). Layer an additive or override pose (raise/tuck). Keep gameplay aim ray separate from the visual retract.
