# FX-38 · Photosensitivity Review

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Haptics, Accessibility, and Cross-sensory Information

## What you see

Check full-screen white flashes, saturated red flashes, high-contrast repeating patterns, and flicker formed by overlapping effects—not just isolated ability assets.

## Not to be confused with

Adding a warning, staying below a particular frequency, or lowering a brightness slider does not guarantee safety for everyone. XAG lists testing conditions, but this handbook does not extract individual numbers as universal safety thresholds or describe passing a risk test as a medical guarantee.

## Use it for

Remove or reduce potentially reaction-triggering content early, and use appropriate analysis tools to check actual gameplay output.

## Tunable parameters

full-frame capture · flash analysis · red-flash review · pattern contrast · overlap scenario · mitigation variant · tool report

## Request prompt

Capture 30 minutes of final gameplay output at 60 fps, including a worst case with six overlapping effects; analyze full-screen flashes, red flashes, and high-contrast patterns with an appropriate tool, manually review every flagged interval, and produce the original plus two reduced-risk variants without claiming absolute safety.

## Implementation hint

Analyze captured final gameplay output, including overlapping effects and post-processing, with an appropriate photosensitivity risk tool. Review flagged intervals manually, reduce or replace risky presentation, and avoid treating a passing result as a medical guarantee.
