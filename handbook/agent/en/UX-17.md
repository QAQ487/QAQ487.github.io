# UX-17 · Damage indicator / Directional damage indicator

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** Motion and Interaction Feedback

## What you see

A red arc on one side of the screen, a health decrease, and a cry of pain occur together, helping you immediately recognize a hit and the threat’s direction.

## Not to be confused with

Do not confuse this with a hit marker, which confirms that you hit a target. A red arc indicates a threat direction, not necessarily an enemy’s precise location.

## Use it for

A red arc on one side of the screen, a health decrease, and a cry of pain occur together, helping you immediately recognize a hit and the threat’s direction.

## Tunable parameters

hit event · relative direction · arc width · health delta · audio cue · decay

## Request prompt

Create directional damage feedback using 8 incoming sectors, a 70-degree arc lasting 0.8 seconds, an 18-point health loss, and a pain sound; accumulate intensity for 3 hits within 1 second without implying an exact enemy position or a hit marker.

## Implementation hint

Convert incoming-hit direction into a screen-relative sector and combine it with health and audio or haptic feedback. Use the cue for approximate threat direction only, with decay and overlap rules for multiple attackers.
