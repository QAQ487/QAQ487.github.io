# UI-13 · Toast / Notification popup

**Aliases:** Toast; Notification toast; Snackbar; Feed message; Pop-up notification

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** HUD & interface types

## What you see

A small message slides into a screen corner (e.g. "+3 Wood", "Quest updated"), stays for about 2–3 s, then slides away or fades on its own; several stack into a column, with new ones pushing old ones along; the player does not have to press anything and the game is not paused.

## Not to be confused with

Not a modal dialog (needs a confirm press and blocks input); a toast never interrupts play. Not a reward pop (UI-06, a large centre-screen celebration). Not a tooltip (UI-12, which only appears when the player points at something).

## Use it for

Pickup logs, quest updates, achievements, save complete, network status. Tells the player what happened without asking them to deal with it now.

## Tunable parameters

lifetime · entrance/exit motion · stacking direction & spacing · max visible · merge rule for duplicates · priority/colour by type

## Request prompt

Add toast notifications to the top-right of your game: each 14 px tall and 96 px wide, sliding in from off the right edge with ease-out over 0.2 s, staying 2.5 s, then fading over 0.25 s; at most 4 at once, the newest on top pushing older ones down 16 px over 0.15 s; repeats of the same kind (e.g. "+1 Wood") within 1 s merge into "+3 Wood" and reset the timer instead of adding a new line.

## Implementation hint

Keep a queue of {text, icon, born, slotY}; each frame tween slotY toward index*spacing, compute alpha from age, and drop items past lifetime. Use unscaled time so toasts still expire during slow motion. Draw in screen space at a fixed anchor with safe-area margins.
