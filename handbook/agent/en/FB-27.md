# FB-27 · Part break / Component severing

**Aliases:** Part break; Breakable parts; Tail cut; Armor stripping; Component removal; 部位破壞; 斷尾; 裝甲剝落; 部位破壊; パーツ破壊; 尻尾切断

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

When one part of an enemy (tail, horn, armour plate, machine component) takes enough focused damage, it flashes and cracks or sparks, then snaps off as a physics debris piece that flies, bounces and stays on the ground. The socket reveals a strongly contrasting inner layer (cut surface, inner machinery, glowing core). From then on the enemy silhouette is permanently different — a missing tail or plate stays readable for the rest of the fight.

## Not to be confused with

Not permanence (FB-12 — marks and bodies left on the ground or walls). Not stance break (ACT-41 — a temporary off-balance state that recovers). This card is a permanent change to the enemy's body. Not a whole-object shatter (VFX-19 — the entire object breaks into many pieces).

## Use it for

Large monsters and bosses, mechanical enemies, fights that must show the player that hitting this spot works.

## Tunable parameters

pre-break crack / flash 0.1–0.2 s · debris impulse along hit direction · debris lifetime (persist vs fade) · inner-layer colour contrast · burst particles · which parts are breakable (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add part breaks to a large enemy. When a designated part (e.g. tail, back plate) accumulates enough hits: flash white with cracks for 0.1–0.2 s, then detach the part as one physics debris piece launched along the hit direction that bounces and stays on the ground; swap the socket to a stump that shows an inner layer contrasting strongly with the shell, and emit one burst of sparks or chips. The enemy keeps the broken silhouette until the fight ends while animation plays normally. Accept: no break below the threshold; after the break the silhouette is clearly different and the debris does not sink into the floor.

## Implementation hint

Author the enemy as a base mesh plus detachable part meshes, each with a stump variant. Track hits per part socket; when a part's threshold is crossed, hide the part mesh, show the stump, spawn a rigid-body debris copy at the socket transform with an impulse along the hit direction, and emit a burst. Keep the stump skinned to the skeleton so animation is unaffected, and persist the broken flag for the rest of the encounter.
