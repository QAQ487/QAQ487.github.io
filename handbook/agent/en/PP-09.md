# PP-09 · Screen-space distortion

**Aliases:** Refraction distortion; Heat distortion; Displacement post-process; Screen warp

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Blur & distortion

## What you see

Part of the image (or all of it) is pushed, bent or wobbled, as if seen through glass, water or hot air; the objects themselves don’t deform — the rendered image is resampled with an offset.

## Not to be confused with

Not heat haze (ENV-17, one environmental application of this technique fixed above a heat source) nor a shockwave (VFX-06, which can be built with it but is a one-off ring effect). This entry is the general technique: read the rendered frame and offset its UVs.

## Use it for

Transparent shields, underwater or drunk wobble, portals, the outline of invisible characters, explosion blasts, heat haze.

## Tunable parameters

offset strength (px) · distortion buffer resolution · normal/noise texture · mask shape · chromatic split · rim highlight

## Request prompt

Add a screen-distortion layer to the game: effects draw offset vectors into a half-resolution distortion buffer (RG channels, ±8 px), and the final composite resamples the frame by that offset; example: a 28 px-radius transparent bubble moving along a path that magnifies like a lens, offset peaking at 6 px mid-radius and 0 at the centre and rim, with a 1 px white rim highlight; clear the distortion buffer every frame.

## Implementation hint

Copy the scene colour to a texture (it cannot be read while bound as the render target). Effects render offset vectors (from a normal map or analytic shape) additively into an RG16F target; composite: out = scene.Sample(uv + offset.rg*strength/screenSize).
