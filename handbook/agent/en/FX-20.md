# FX-20 · Randomization / Variation

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Repeated footsteps or gunshots are not identical each time; samples, pitch, or volume vary.

## Not to be confused with

Changing samples and changing pitch are different kinds of variation. Random selection does not guarantee that consecutive repeats are prevented; that depends on rules such as selection without replacement.

## Use it for

Reduce mechanical repetition while preserving the event’s identity.

## Tunable parameters

sample pool · no-repeat window · pitch range · gain range · selection weight · cooldown · identity anchor

## Request prompt

Prepare eight stone-footstep samples, exclude the three most recent choices from selection, vary pitch within ±3% and gain within ±1.5 dB, and verify over 20 consecutive steps that the material identity remains consistent.

## Implementation hint

Select from a curated sample pool and vary pitch or gain within restrained ranges. Track recent choices when consecutive repetition is undesirable, while retaining common spectral features that identify the event.
