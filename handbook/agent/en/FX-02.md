# FX-02 · Trails / Ribbons

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

A continuous strip follows a sword tip, missile, or moving point of light, showing its previous path.

## Not to be confused with

A dispersing particle smoke trail is not necessarily a ribbon. A continuous sword slash may also be a prebuilt mesh; appearance alone cannot establish how it was generated.

## Use it for

Communicate movement direction, swing arcs, speed, and a sense of attack coverage.

## Tunable parameters

sample interval · ribbon width · history length · UV tiling · taper · fade time

## Request prompt

Build a 0.35-second sword-tip trail sampled every 8 ms, taper its width from 12 cm to zero, and fade the tail over 0.18 seconds so the full swing arc remains readable.

## Implementation hint

Sample a moving attachment point and connect recent positions into a continuous strip. Taper and fade older samples while keeping the rendered path distinct from separately emitted smoke particles.
