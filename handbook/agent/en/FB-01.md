# FB-01 · Hit flash

**Aliases:** White flash; Damage flash; Flash on hit

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Hit & damage reaction

## What you see

On the frame of impact the whole sprite turns solid white (or red) for roughly 2–6 frames, then snaps back. The silhouette is unchanged; only the colour is overridden.

## Not to be confused with

Not invincibility blink (FB-03, an on/off flicker lasting a while) nor status tint (FB-05, a lingering translucent colour). A hit flash is one short, full-coverage pulse.

## Use it for

Confirms "that hit landed", especially in busy scenes or with tiny sprites; best stacked with hitstop and knockback.

## Tunable parameters

duration 0.05–0.12 s · colour (white / red / team colour) · easing (snap vs fade-out) · max re-trigger rate

## Request prompt

In your game, add a hit flash when an enemy or NPC takes damage: override the whole sprite with pure white for 0.08 s (~5 frames), then snap back; bosses use 0.12 s. Trigger it in sync with the existing hitstop and do not change the sprite silhouette.

## Implementation hint

Pixel shader uniform flashAmount (0..1): out.rgb = lerp(tex.rgb, flashColor, flashAmount * tex.a). Set per-draw via a small constant buffer or per-instance data; decay on the CPU each tick. Keep alpha from the texture so the silhouette is preserved.
