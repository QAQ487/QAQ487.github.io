# FB-35 · Copy ability

**Aliases:** Ability absorb; Power copy; Weapon copy; Ability steal; Soul absorb; Ability star; 複製能力; 吸收能力; 奪取能力; 能力星; コピー能力; 能力コピー; アビリティスター

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

After the character swallows or defeats an enemy that has a special power, the action pauses for a beat, the character flashes and spins round, and a new hat appears on its head, a new outfit goes on or its whole palette changes; swallow a fire-breathing enemy, say, and it gets a crown of flame and turns red. From then on the attack button does the same kind of move as that enemy, and the ability icon in the corner of the screen changes to match. A heavy hit knocks the ability out of the character as a star or orb that bounces along the ground and disappears after a few seconds; the hat goes with it and the character is back to its plain look, but it can grab or inhale the star again before it vanishes.

## Not to be confused with

Not possession (ACT-53, where the player drives the other body). Not an ordinary weapon swap (picked from a menu, with the character's look not tied to where the power came from). Not inhale and spit (FB-34, which uses the target only as ammunition).

## Use it for

Making enemy types double as a source of powers, reading the current power from the character's look at a glance, encouraging players to try different powers on puzzles, taking a boss's weapon after beating it.

## Tunable parameters

ability tag per enemy · transform beat (pause, flash, spin) · per-ability hat, outfit or palette · move-set swap · corner icon · drop on heavy hit: bouncing ability star, lifetime & blink, re-absorb · voluntary discard (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add a copy ability. When the character swallows or defeats an enemy with an ability tag, play a short transform beat (pause, flash, spin), give the character that ability's own hat, outfit or palette, replace the attack button's moves with the ability's moves and show the ability icon in the corner. On a heavy hit, knock the ability out as an ability star that bounces along the ground and vanishes after a few seconds, returning the character to its normal look; touching or inhaling the star before it vanishes gets the ability back. Accept: every ability can be told apart from the character's look without the icon, and nothing of the ability remains once the star has gone.

## Implementation hint

Keep a current-ability slot on the character. On a qualifying swallow or defeat, play a short transform sequence, then attach the ability's cosmetic (hat mesh, outfit swap or palette shift) and replace the attack bindings with the ability's move set. On a heavy hit or a discard input, clear the slot, spawn a bouncing pickup that stores the ability id with a lifetime and a blink before expiry, and remove the cosmetic. Touching or inhaling the pickup restores the ability.
