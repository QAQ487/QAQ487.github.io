# UX-32 · Gating / Hard gate

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Access Rules, Returning, and Learning

## What you see

A door opens only after all enemies are defeated, or passage is withheld until a mechanism finishes. Direct restrictions control access to areas or progression.

## Not to be confused with

A gate need not be a physical door: barriers or blocking NPCs can serve the same purpose. Do not confuse gating with signposting: one tells you where to go; the other restricts whether you can go there.

## Use it for

A door opens only after all enemies are defeated, or passage is withheld until a mechanism finishes. Direct restrictions control access to areas or progression.

## Tunable parameters

blocking condition · hard barrier · progress count · unlock event · feedback · persistence

## Request prompt

Build a hard gate that completely blocks passage until all 4 enemies are cleared, showing Clear enemies 2/4 on approach; open it over 1.2 seconds at zero, persist the unlock, and prevent rolling or edge bypasses.

## Implementation hint

Represent the gate as a rule that fully blocks passage until an explicit condition becomes true. Show condition progress and failure feedback, then transition the barrier once and persist the unlocked state as required.
