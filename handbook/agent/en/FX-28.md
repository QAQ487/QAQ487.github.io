# FX-28 · Audio Hierarchy / Mix Separation

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Important warnings and dialogue are not completely masked by ambience and music. Settings also allow separate adjustment of music, voice, and effects.

## Not to be confused with

Louder does not necessarily mean clearer. Not every instance of making room in a mix is sidechain ducking; timbre, arrangement, or manually set volume hierarchies may do the work.

## Use it for

Make decision-relevant information audible and let players adjust it to their needs.

## Tunable parameters

bus priority · dialogue headroom · frequency slot · duck amount · attack time · release time · user sliders

## Request prompt

Create separate music, voice, and effects controls; when a critical warning plays, lower music by 4 dB within 80 ms and recover over 500 ms, preserve 6 dB of dialogue peak headroom, and verify intelligibility in five combat captures.

## Implementation hint

Organize voice, warnings, effects, ambience, and music into controllable groups with explicit priorities and headroom. Create separation through arrangement, spectrum, and level before applying selective ducking where needed.
