# FX-04 · Flipbook / Sprite-sheet Animation

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

The internal shape of a flame or explosion evolves continuously, as though playing a short predrawn or precomputed animation, rather than merely enlarging and fading one image.

## Not to be confused with

A flipbook organizes frames over time; a sprite is a carrier. They are not mutually exclusive categories. Procedural materials can also produce similar results.

## Use it for

Condense complex smoke and fire changes into texture sequences for particles to play.

## Tunable parameters

frame count · playback rate · atlas layout · frame blending · lifetime · random start

## Request prompt

Create a 64-frame explosion from an 8×8 atlas, play it at 32 fps for 2 seconds, and scale the carrier only from 0.4 m to 2.2 m while ensuring the internal flame silhouette changes every frame.

## Implementation hint

Store the evolving shape as ordered frames in a texture atlas and advance the frame index over particle lifetime. Keep frame playback independent from scale and opacity curves so the interior visibly changes instead of merely enlarging.
