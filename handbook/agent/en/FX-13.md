# FX-13 · VFX Anticipation / Attack Charge-up

**Aliases:** Anticipation / Wind-up; Charge-up VFX; Attack wind-up VFX

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Before a burst, an effect contracts, gathers light, establishes a direction, or introduces a preparatory sound. You sense that something is about to happen.

## Not to be confused with

Anticipation is preparatory presentation; startup is the gameplay interval before an attack’s hit detection becomes active. Interpret wind-up in context: preparatory presentation does not necessarily cover the entire startup. A telegraph is a warning signal and can include animation, sound, and ground cues, not just particles. Compare ACT-09 (animation anticipation) and ACT-18 (attack startup).

## Use it for

Build expectation while communicating when to react.

## Tunable parameters

anticipation duration · contraction amount · light buildup · directional cue · preparatory sound · release frame

## Request prompt

Create a 0.8-second anticipation for a linear energy blast: contract light from 1.2 m to 0.25 m during the first 0.5 seconds, raise brightness to 180% over the final 0.3 seconds, and add a short cue 100 ms before release.

## Implementation hint

Stage a visible and audible preparation before release, such as contraction, gathering light, or direction establishment. Align its release with gameplay timing while allowing the presentation interval to differ from the complete startup interval.
