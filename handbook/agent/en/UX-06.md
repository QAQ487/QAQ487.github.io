# UX-06 · Focus indicator / Focus highlight

**Aliases:** Focus vs selected; Equipment slot states; 焦點與已選; フォーカスと選択

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** On-screen Information and Menus

## What you see

The focus ring (what you are pointing at) is separate from the equipped/selected mark: the ring can move to slot two while the check stays on slot one; the same slot can also show press squash or a disabled lock.

## Not to be confused with

Focus ≠ selected. After a modal closes, restore the prior focus, or the nearest enabled item if it was removed.

## Use it for

The focus ring (what you are pointing at) is separate from the equipped/selected mark: the ring can move to slot two while the check stays on slot one; the same slot can also show press squash or a disabled lock.

## Tunable parameters

focus ring · selected / equipped mark · pressed squash · disabled lock · hover · contrast · focus restore

## Request prompt

On one equipment slot show four states: a white focus ring, a corner check for equipped, a brief press squash, and a lock with reason when disabled. Focus and equipped may coexist but must not look the same; restore focus after a modal, or the nearest enabled slot if the original was removed. Keep contrast readable on a dark panel.

## Implementation hint

On one equipment slot, show distinct tokens: white/cyan focus ring, corner check for equipped/selected, brief press squash, and a lock plus reason when disabled. States may stack (focused+equipped) without looking identical. Restore the prior focus after a modal closes; if that item was removed, select the nearest remaining enabled slot.
