# UX-26 · Leading lines / Directional composition

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

Tracks, cables, or floor seams extend toward a doorway, visually pointing in its direction. This is a common search term, not an assertion of a reliable psychological law.

## Not to be confused with

Players do not necessarily look or walk along these lines automatically. The Level Design Book specifically criticizes overclaims that leading lines subconsciously control routes. Actual paths, traversability, and relevance to objectives matter more.

## Use it for

Tracks, cables, or floor seams extend toward a doorway, visually pointing in its direction. This is a common search term, not an assertion of a reliable psychological law.

## Tunable parameters

floor seams · rails · destination framing · traversable path · obstacle · validation

## Request prompt

Use 4 floor seams and 2 cables to point compositionally toward an exit, but place an obstacle requiring a 6 m detour to the right; reinforce the traversable route and exit separately rather than assuming players subconsciously follow the lines.

## Implementation hint

Compose lines and spatial forms so they visually point toward a relevant destination, then validate them against actual traversability and objective logic. Treat the composition as a supporting cue, never as a guarantee that attention or movement will follow it.
