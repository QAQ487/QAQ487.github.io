# UX-28 · Vista / Overlook；Approach

**Group:** Visual guidance & wayfinding
**Subcategory:** Spatial Recognition and Guidance

## What you see

Leaving a narrow passage, you first see the plaza, enemies, and exit from above before entering the space. A vista helps players form a spatial understanding in advance; an approach is the route toward the area that presents such a view.

## Not to be confused with

Not every beautiful view serves navigation; a distant view may exist purely for atmosphere. What matters here is whether you can identify entrances, objectives, and how to get down into the area.

## Use it for

Leaving a narrow passage, you first see the plaza, enemies, and exit from above before entering the space. A vista helps players form a spatial understanding in advance; an approach is the route toward the area that presents such a view.

## Tunable parameters

overlook height · preview targets · approach route · entrance visibility · descent · spatial plan

## Request prompt

Design a 20 m narrow approach to a 6 m-high overlook where players can preview 3 enemies, 1 exit, and a descent ramp on the left; allow at least 2 seconds to observe before entry and make both the entrance and descent legible.

## Implementation hint

Reveal the next space from an approach or overlook before entry, framing the objective, hazards, and route into the area. Ensure the preview supports a usable spatial plan rather than functioning only as scenery.
