# FB-02 · Damage numbers

**Aliases:** Floating combat text; Damage popups; Pop-up numbers

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** UI feedback

## What you see

A number pops out above the target on hit, drifts upward and fades; usually it overshoots in scale first, and crits are bigger and a different colour.

## Not to be confused with

Not a combo counter (FB-18, a running total pinned to the screen corner) or a HUD health bar. Damage numbers live in world space, follow the target and are short-lived.

## Use it for

Shows the value of every hit and its variation (crits, resistances, green for healing); great for RPG or tower-defence-style number growth.

## Tunable parameters

lifetime 0.6–1.0 s · rise distance · scale overshoot · colour per damage type · stacking offset · font

## Request prompt

Add floating damage numbers to the game: on each hit spawn a pixel-font number above the target that scales 150%→100% in 0.1 s, then rises 12 px and fades over 0.7 s. Crits are gold and one size larger; heals are green with a "+". Offset simultaneous numbers slightly left/right so they do not overlap.

## Implementation hint

Spawn screen-space text quads from world position projected each frame (or keep them in world space with a billboard). Use a bitmap pixel font atlas; batch all numbers into one dynamic vertex buffer. Alpha and scale from a per-instance age value.
