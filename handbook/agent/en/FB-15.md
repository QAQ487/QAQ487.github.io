# FB-15 · Muzzle flash

**Aliases:** Gun flash; Firing flash

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Combat effect feedback

## What you see

At the moment of firing a bright star/cone flash appears at the muzzle for 1–3 frames and briefly lights the surroundings; only then do you see the projectile or smoke.

## Not to be confused with

Not an explosion (VFX-09) nor a tracer (VFX-08). A muzzle flash lives only at the firing point and is extremely short.

## Use it for

Gives shots power and rhythm and doubles as a light source in the dark; also works for wands and ballistae.

## Tunable parameters

frames 1–3 · size · random rotation/sprite variant · light radius & duration · smoke follow-up

## Request prompt

In your game, each time a crossbow tower fires show a 2-frame yellow-white star flash at the muzzle (big then small, randomly rotated 0/45°), add a 0.05 s warm point light with 24 px radius, then a wisp of grey smoke.

## Implementation hint

Additive sprite (Blend: One/One) spawned for N frames; register a transient point light into the 2D light buffer (PIX-21) if lighting is enabled.
