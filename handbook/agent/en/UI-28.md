# UI-28 · Directional guard indicator

**Aliases:** Directional guard; Directional block; Stance direction indicator; Guard direction reticle; Directional combat UI; 方向架勢格擋; 方向防禦指示; 三向架勢; 方向ガード; 構え方向インジケーター

**Group:** UI & HUD
**Subcategory:** HUD & interface types

## What you see

While locked on, a three-segment stance marker (left, top, right) sits around the opponent. As the player picks a guard direction with the stick, the matching segment lights cyan and the character's weapon swings to that side. When the opponent winds up an attack from a direction, that segment of the same marker turns red and flashes. Moving the guard to the same direction blocks it — sparks at the contact and the red segment goes out; a different direction gets hit.

## Not to be confused with

Not a threat direction ring (UI-27, which tells you from which bearing an off-screen enemy attacks, nothing to do with guard direction). Not an attack telegraph (NAV-04, the area about to be hit on the ground). Not a perfect parry (FB-26, which is about timing, not direction). This card is the match-the-direction cue where both attack and defence choose a side.

## Use it for

Sword duels, one-on-one melee, combat systems where you read the direction of the opponent's attack.

## Tunable parameters

direction count (3–5) · marker anchor (around opponent / screen centre) · own-guard colour · incoming colour & flash rate · flash lead time before impact · block spark · shown only while locked on (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add a directional guard indicator to one-on-one melee. While locked on, draw a three-segment marker (left, top, right) around the opponent: the player's chosen direction lights cyan and the weapon pose follows it. When the opponent starts an attack, flash the matching segment red until the attack resolves. On resolve, if the player's guard matches the attack direction it is a block — sparks at the contact and the red segment goes out — otherwise a hit. Accept: one attack from each of the three directions; matching blocks, mismatching hits, and the marker always agrees with the weapon pose.

## Implementation hint

Store a guard direction per fighter, set from the stick while locked on. Draw a segmented marker anchored to the opponent in screen space; highlight the player's own segment and drive the upper-body guard pose from the same value. When an attack starts, tag it with a direction and flash that segment from wind-up until resolution. On impact compare directions: equal → block feedback; otherwise hit.
