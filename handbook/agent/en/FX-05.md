# FX-05 · Mesh Particles

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Rotating rocks, ice crystals, and fragments reveal three-dimensional sides and changing silhouettes, rather than just camera-facing images.

## Not to be confused with

A three-dimensional appearance does not imply rigid-body physics; particles may follow preset velocities. Ordinary scene objects can also imitate particle effects.

## Use it for

Create debris, crystals, and geometric magical forms with thickness.

## Tunable parameters

mesh set · spawn count · angular velocity · scale range · ballistic arc · lifetime · collision mode

## Request prompt

On a stone-wall hit, spawn 18 three-dimensional fragments selected from four meshes, launch them at 3–7 m/s with 180–540°/s rotation, and let them fall and fade within 1.4 seconds without rigid-body collision.

## Implementation hint

Spawn lightweight mesh instances with independent translation, rotation, and scale curves. Use preset ballistic motion unless actual collision response is required; visible thickness alone should not imply rigid-body simulation.
