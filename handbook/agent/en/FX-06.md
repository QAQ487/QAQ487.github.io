# FX-06 · Decals / Surface Marks

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Bullet holes, scorch marks, and ground symbols adhere to existing surfaces, looking printed onto a wall or floor rather than floating in space.

## Not to be confused with

A flat transparent plane or a change to the ground material can look similar. “On the ground” does not automatically mean a decal renderer was used.

## Use it for

Leave a positional record of an impact or mark a ground state.

## Tunable parameters

projection size · surface offset · normal alignment · lifetime · opacity · receiver mask · edge feather

## Request prompt

After each fireball hit, leave a 0.9 m scorch mark aligned to the wall or floor normal, offset it 2 mm from the surface, feather the edge by 8 cm, hold it for 6 seconds, then fade it over 1 second.

## Implementation hint

Project or place a surface-aligned mark only on eligible receivers, with a small depth offset to avoid flicker. Fade or remove it according to gameplay state without assuming that every surface mark requires a dedicated decal renderer.
