# FX-23 · Distance Attenuation

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Moving away from a source reduces its volume with distance. A nearby region may retain maximum volume.

## Not to be confused with

Lower volume can also result from player settings, obstruction, or mixing. Volume alone does not establish distance, and not all games follow physically realistic attenuation.

## Use it for

Communicate distance and limit a sound source’s reach.

## Tunable parameters

minimum distance · maximum distance · rolloff curve · near-field plateau · floor gain · source priority

## Request prompt

Set generator attenuation to hold 0 dB from 0–3 m, fall smoothly to −18 dB by 20 m, reach −36 dB at 35 m, and mute beyond that; walk-test at 1 m intervals to catch abrupt level changes.

## Implementation hint

Evaluate source-listener distance against a designed attenuation curve with an optional near-field plateau and distant floor. Tune the curve for gameplay reach rather than assuming physically exact falloff.
