# FB-29 · Delayed slash detonation / Lingering rift

**Aliases:** Delayed slash; Lingering rift; Delayed cut; Spatial rift slash; Sheathe detonation; 延遲斬擊; 空間裂痕; 次元斬; 遅延斬撃; 空間の裂け目; 納刀で炸裂

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Combat effect feedback

## What you see

After a burst of very fast slashes, or a high-speed dash through a group, thin rift lines hang in the air where the blade passed, with slight distortion around them; enemies they crossed only twitch. A short hush follows, the background audio ducks, and the character sheathes or turns away. On the click of the sheathe, every rift shatters like glass at once, and every enemy touched by a rift is hit together and launched, with a heavy low-frequency boom.

## Not to be confused with

Not slash VFX (VFX-07 — the swing arc at the moment of the swing, which vanishes right away). Not a multi-hit attack (the weapon really connects several times). Not damage over time (spread evenly over a duration). This card is a delayed hit left in space by the swing and detonated all at once on the recovery key frame.

## Use it for

Iaido-style draws, super moves, ultra-fast dash attacks, any move that needs the cut-first-burst-later payoff.

## Tunable parameters

rift delay 0.4–0.8 s (tied to the recovery key frame) · distortion strength · pre-burst audio duck · detonation hitstop 8–12 f · shatter shard count · interrupt behaviour (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add a delayed-slash finisher. During the slashes, leave glowing rift lines along the blade-tip path (with slight screen distortion) that hang still; crossed enemies play only a light flinch. Duck the background audio for the next 0.4–0.8 s while the character sheathes; on the sheathe-click anim event, shatter all rifts at once and hit every enemy they touched in one go with an 8–12 frame hitstop, a launch, and a deep boom. Accept: several enemies crossed by different rifts are all launched on the same frame; if the sheathe is interrupted, the rifts fade without detonating.

## Implementation hint

During active frames, sample blade-tip positions into a polyline and build a ribbon mesh with a distortion / emissive material; record every enemy the blade overlapped. On the recovery anim event (sheathe click), shatter the ribbons (spawn shards along the polyline) and apply the deferred hit to all recorded targets in the same frame, with hitstop, shake and the low-frequency cue. On interrupt, fade the ribbons without detonating.
