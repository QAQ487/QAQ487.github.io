# FX-14 · Timing / Rhythm / Contrast

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

Notice whether an effect builds slowly and suddenly bursts or expands at a constant rate; whether combo hits have distinct gaps or merge into one mass.

## Not to be confused with

More particles do not automatically create more impact. Display duration, the speed of shape changes, and empty intervals all affect readability. Timing ratios from a particular case study are not universal recipes.

## Use it for

Separate preparation, burst, and resolution, giving major abilities a different rhythm from ordinary attacks.

## Tunable parameters

build duration · burst duration · hold interval · decay duration · hit spacing · speed curve · silence gap

## Request prompt

Design a three-hit combo with 0.28 seconds between hits: give the first two hits a 0.08-second burst and 0.16-second decay, then hold for 0.18 seconds before the third hit peaks in 0.04 seconds and resolves over 0.6 seconds.

## Implementation hint

Divide the effect into preparation, peak, and resolution phases with deliberately contrasting rates. Use gaps and hit spacing to separate beats instead of increasing density uniformly.
