# ACT-16 · Secondary Action

**Group:** Animation, movement & physics
**Subcategory:** Animation Principles and Action Readability

## What you see

A character clenches a fist while walking: walking is the main action, while the smaller gesture conveys tension.

## Not to be confused with

Every movement of an auxiliary bone being a narrative secondary action. Passively moving hair is more often explained through overlap or follow through.

## Use it for

Support the main action with personality and emotion without stealing focus.

## Tunable parameters

primary action · secondary gesture · gesture amplitude · focus hierarchy · emotional intent

## Request prompt

Add a 300 ms fist-clench secondary action within a 2.4 s walk cycle, reaching 70% of a full clench while keeping the gait and body silhouette clearly readable.

## Implementation hint

Layer a restrained expressive gesture onto the main action, ensuring its timing and silhouette support rather than obscure the primary read. Use the gesture to communicate a specific emotion or intention.
