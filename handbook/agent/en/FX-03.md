# FX-03 · Afterimages / Ghost Trails

**Aliases:** Temporal ghosting; History smearing; Disocclusion artifacts; 衝刺殘像; Dash afterimage; 殘影; 時間性殘影

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

After a character moves away, fading outlines or poses remain at earlier positions, like snapshots of their past self.

## Not to be confused with

Motion blur smears movement; afterimages usually have more distinctly repeated silhouettes. Both can appear together, and afterimages do not necessarily duplicate the character mesh.

## Use it for

Emphasize dashes, teleportation, acceleration, or special states. A dash that leaves several fading, clearly separated translucent poses is an intentional afterimage. A translucent tail from stale history, or a brief smear on newly revealed background, is an accident and not this effect. (Former entry REN-41, “Temporal ghosting”, is merged here only as that contrast.)

## Tunable parameters

snapshot interval · ghost count · pose retention · tint · opacity decay · silhouette offset

## Request prompt

Add five afterimages to a 6 m dash, retaining one pose every 70 ms; start the first ghost at 55% opacity and fade each over 0.4 seconds, with clear gaps between silhouettes.

## Implementation hint

Capture discrete character silhouettes or simplified pose proxies at timed positions, then fade each independently. Preserve visible gaps between ghosts so the result reads as repeated poses rather than continuous motion blur.
