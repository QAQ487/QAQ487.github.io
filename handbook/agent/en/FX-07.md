# FX-07 · Distortion / Refraction

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

As heat haze, a shock ring, or transparent energy passes, scenery behind it appears displaced or bent, rather than simply overlaid with color.

## Not to be confused with

Distortion may be a stylized screen-space offset, not physically accurate refraction. Camera shake moves the whole viewpoint rather than deforming a local part of the background.

## Use it for

Convey heat, transparent media, energy density, or impact.

## Tunable parameters

distortion strength · screen radius · normal map speed · falloff · duration · chromatic offset

## Request prompt

Create a shock-distortion ring that expands from a 0.2 m to 2.5 m radius over 0.45 seconds; cap displacement at 1.5% of screen width and reduce it to zero at the rim during the final 0.12 seconds without moving the camera.

## Implementation hint

Render a localized displacement field that samples and offsets the already rendered background. Limit the effect spatially and temporally so it reads as local heat or refraction rather than whole-camera movement.
