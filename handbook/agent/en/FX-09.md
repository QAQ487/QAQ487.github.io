# FX-09 · Impact Burst / Hit Spark

**Group:** VFX & particles
**Subcategory:** Realtime VFX

## What you see

At the moment of contact, spikes, bright points, or debris suddenly burst out locally and quickly disappear.

## Not to be confused with

A blade flashing during a swing is not hit confirmation. Hit stop is a brief temporal pause; an impact burst is a visual form. Both can be combined.

## Use it for

Identify the contact point and make the instant of impact clear, helping viewers distinguish an attack being launched from an actual hit.

## Tunable parameters

contact position · burst duration · spike count · debris count · peak brightness · scale curve · material response

## Request prompt

On a confirmed melee hit, spawn 10 radial spikes and six debris pieces at the contact point; make the main burst reach a 0.7 m maximum in 30 ms and end within 0.16 seconds, with no burst on a miss.

## Implementation hint

Spawn a short, localized burst at the confirmed contact point, with its main shape peaking immediately and resolving quickly. Keep attack-swing decoration separate so the burst functions as unambiguous hit confirmation.
