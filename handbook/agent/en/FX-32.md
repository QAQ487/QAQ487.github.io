# FX-32 · Haptic Feedback / Rumble

**Aliases:** Controller vibration; Tactile feedback; 手把震動; コントローラー振動

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Haptics, Accessibility, and Cross-sensory Information

## What you see

A controller vibrates on hits, landings, or damage, taking the event beyond the screen and speakers.

## Not to be confused with

Stronger vibration does not mean clearer information, and vibration is not trigger resistance. Device support varies; do not assume every player can feel it.

## Use it for

Add tactile sensation and provide an additional information channel. Offer intensity control and a full off switch. Not everyone enables vibration and not every device has motors, so essential information must never rely on vibration alone — pair it with a visual or audio cue. (Former entry UX-19, “Controller vibration and tactile feedback”, is merged here.)

## Tunable parameters

amplitude · duration · motor balance · attack · release · event priority · device fallback

## Request prompt

For a landing from 3 m, play a 180 ms vibration that rises to 65% intensity in 20 ms, holds for 80 ms, and releases to zero over 80 ms; preserve visual and audio confirmation when vibration is unsupported or disabled.

## Implementation hint

Trigger a bounded haptic envelope for the event and adapt it to the capabilities exposed by each device. Treat haptics as an additional channel, preserving equivalent visual or audio feedback when unavailable.
