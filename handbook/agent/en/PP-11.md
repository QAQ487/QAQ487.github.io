# PP-11 · Lens dirt

**Aliases:** Lens dirt mask; Dirt mask; Bloom dirt; Dirty lens

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Lens simulation

## What you see

When a strong light (sun, explosion, lamp) is on screen, dust, fingerprints, water marks and specks on the lens light up; the brighter and closer the light, the more visible, and with no strong light they vanish entirely.

## Not to be confused with

Not a lens flare (REN-35, ghost spots lined up between the light and the screen centre); dirt stays fixed on the “lens” and doesn’t move with the light — only its brightness follows it. Not a vignette (PP-01, constant edge darkening).

## Use it for

Selling backlight and explosion impact, a realistic or camera-POV texture, space and sci-fi scenes.

## Tunable parameters

dirt texture · intensity · bloom threshold · tint · which bloom mip feeds it

## Request prompt

Add lens dirt to the game: author a fullscreen dirt mask (soft round blotches, hexagonal specks, a few scratches, mostly black) and during bloom compositing add bloom × dirtMask × intensity 1.5; dirt only lights up near areas where bloom exceeds the threshold (luminance > 1.0); intensity 0 disables it.

## Implementation hint

In the bloom composite: dirtContribution = bloomWide.rgb * dirtTex.Sample(screenUV).rgb * dirtIntensity, using a wide (low-mip) bloom level so a bright source lights dirt far from itself; out = scene + bloom + dirtContribution. The dirt texture is in screen space, not world space.
