# FX-37 · Reduced Motion / Visual Effects Options

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Haptics, Accessibility, and Cross-sensory Information

## What you see

Players can reduce nonessential motion such as camera shake and motion blur while retaining visibility of aiming, hazards, and object positions.

## Not to be confused with

Reduced motion does not mean deleting all character animation, nor is it a photosensitivity mode. After disabling camera shake, check that other hit-confirmation channels remain.

## Use it for

Reduce motion-related discomfort and attention interference, making feedback adjustable to individual needs.

## Tunable parameters

camera shake scale · motion blur toggle · flash reduction · essential motion floor · preview · per-effect override · fallback feedback

## Request prompt

Add a Reduced Motion setting with camera shake at 0%, 25%, 50%, or 100% and an independent motion-blur toggle; at 0% shake, replace hit feedback with a 0.12-second outline and short sound, then test all 10 major attacks.

## Implementation hint

Provide controls that reduce nonessential camera and effect motion while preserving aiming, hazard boundaries, and spatial information. Replace disabled motion-based feedback with stable visual or audio confirmation and test the complete gameplay flow.
