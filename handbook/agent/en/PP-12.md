# PP-12 · Screen glitch (digital corruption, non-flashing)

**Aliases:** 畫面故障; 訊號干擾; 數碼雜訊; Glitch effect; Datamosh look; Block displacement; RGB split glitch; グリッチ

**Group:** Post-processing & screen filters
**Subcategory:** Stylistic filters

## What you see

The image looks like a damaged signal: a few horizontal bands suddenly shift sideways, colour channels separate and occasional blocky noise appears, then everything snaps back. Used for hacking, system damage, unstable time or a character's mind being affected. A safe version only displaces and offsets colour — no full-screen brightness flashing — and caps how often it can trigger.

## Not to be confused with

Not chromatic aberration (REN-36, a fixed lens fringe that does not tear), not a CRT filter (PP-03, persistent scanlines and curvature) and not screen-space distortion (PP-09, smooth refraction waves). A glitch is brief, irregular and blocky.

## Use it for

Hacking or intrusion sequences, damaged robotic characters, memory reading or time rewinds, tech-styled menu transitions.

## Tunable parameters

bands 4–8 · band offset 2–12 px · RGB split 1–3 px · duration 0.15–0.4 s · max 1 trigger/s · luminance change: none (starting points)

## Request prompt

Add a screen-glitch post effect: when triggered, slice the frame into 4–8 horizontal bands of random height, offset each horizontally by 2–12 px and split the red and blue channels 1–3 px in opposite directions, lasting 0.15–0.4 s (all starting values). Do not change overall brightness or alternate light and dark, and trigger at most once per second; provide an off switch in settings and respect the photosensitivity setting (A11Y-04).

## Implementation hint

Fullscreen pass: band = floor((uv.y + seed)*N); offs = (hash(band+seed)-.5)*amp*active; sample r at uv+(offs+split,0), g at uv+(offs,0), b at uv+(offs-split,0). Optional block noise: quantise uv to 8–16 px cells and replace a few cells with a neighbouring cell's colour. Keep mean luminance unchanged; rate-limit triggers.
