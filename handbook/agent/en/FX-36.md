# FX-36 · Visualized Sound Cues / Sound Indicators

**Aliases:** Captions / Visualized Sound Cues; Captions (non-speech sounds); Sound visualization; Directional sound indicator

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Haptics, Accessibility, and Cross-sensory Information

## What you see

Beyond dialogue subtitles, information includes “knocking at the door,” footstep direction, or sound-source identity.

## Not to be confused with

Subtitles mainly represent speech; captions also cover non-speech sounds. A directional arrow indicates direction but not necessarily sound type. Overcrowding such graphics also creates noise.

## Use it for

Make essential information normally conveyed through hearing visible.

## Tunable parameters

sound label · source identity · direction · distance band · display duration · priority · concurrency limit

## Request prompt

When an enemy knocks, display “[8 m, rear right: rapid knocking]” for two seconds and update direction every 100 ms; show at most three sound captions at once and merge same-type footsteps within 0.5 seconds while retaining direction.

## Implementation hint

Convert important non-speech sounds into concise text and directional visuals using source metadata. Prioritize and merge repeated cues to prevent captions from obscuring the scene, and do not rely on arrows alone to communicate sound identity.
