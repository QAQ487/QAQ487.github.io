# FB-38 · Beat-switch platforms

**Aliases:** Beat blocks; Beep blocks; Blinking blocks; Rhythm platforms; Flip panels; Red-blue panels; On-off blocks; 節拍切換平台; 節拍方塊; 紅藍切換方塊; 跳躍翻板; ビートブロック; リズムで切り替わる足場; 切り替えパネル

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Rhythm sync

## What you see

The level has two sets of blocks in different colours (say red and blue): one set is solid, the other is only a dotted outline you can see but not stand on. On every bar of the music the two sets swap, the solid ones turning into outlines and the outlines turning solid. A beat before each switch a 'beep, beep' warning plays and the blocks about to vanish blink; a character standing on one that does not jump off in time drops through. Lights, plants and background props bob gently on the beat too, so the player can feel the rhythm. Variant — jump-flip panels: every time the character jumps, all the panels flip to their other side, so the player plans the route by counting jumps.

## Not to be confused with

Not beat-quantised feedback (FB-21, which snaps effects to the beat while the terrain stays as it is). Not a timed crumbling platform (the countdown starts when you step on it and has nothing to do with the music). Not a rhythm timing window (a rule about when to press). This card is the terrain itself appearing and disappearing on the beat.

## Use it for

Rhythm sections in platformers, making the music a rule of the level, jump routes that need reading ahead, rhythm-action game stages.

## Tunable parameters

two (or more) block sets & colours · solid vs dotted-outline look · switch on chosen beats from the music clock · warning cue & blink one beat ahead · safe push-out when a block appears inside a character · ambient props bob on the beat · jump-flip variant (authoring defaults)

## Request prompt

Add beat-switch platforms. Create two sets of blocks with only one set solid at a time and the other shown as dotted outlines with no collision; on the chosen beats of the music, swap the sets. One beat before each swap, play a warning cue and blink the blocks about to vanish. If a block appears inside a character at the switch, push the character to the nearest safe spot. Optional jump-flip variant: flip all panels on every jump. Accept: the switches stay locked to the music's beats even over long play, and the warning cue and blink always come one beat before the switch.

## Implementation hint

Drive the switch from the music's playback position (beat index from the audio clock), not from frame time, so it never drifts. On each switch beat, toggle collision and look for each set; one beat earlier, play the warning cue and start the blink. When enabling a set, test overlap with characters and resolve it with a minimal push-out. For the jump variant, toggle on the jump event instead of the beat.
