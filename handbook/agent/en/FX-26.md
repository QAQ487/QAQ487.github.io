# FX-26 · Obstruction / Occlusion

**Group:** Audio & haptics
**Subcategory:** Audio

## What you see

Moving behind a pillar or a wall makes a sound muffled and quieter; moving back out restores it.

## Not to be confused with

Terminology varies by system. In this Wwise document on game-defined auxiliary sends, obstruction affects direct sound without affecting reflection sends; occlusion affects both direct sound and sends. Do not impose this distinction on every engine, or treat one obstruction ray as a complete acoustic propagation simulation.

## Use it for

Communicate the obstruction relationship between source and listener.

## Tunable parameters

visibility probes · direct gain · low-pass cutoff · indirect send · attack time · release time · obstacle class

## Request prompt

Check blockage between source and listener with three probes every 100 ms; at full blockage, reduce direct sound by 12 dB and low-pass it to 1.8 kHz, applying over 150 ms and recovering over 350 ms while controlling indirect sound separately.

## Implementation hint

Estimate blockage between source and listener with one or more visibility probes, then drive direct level, filtering, and indirect routing as separate controls. Smooth transitions and avoid presenting a simple probe as complete acoustic propagation.
