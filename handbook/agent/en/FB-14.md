# FB-14 · Pickup magnetism

**Aliases:** Item vacuum; Loot magnet; Auto-collect pull

**Group:** Combat feedback & game feel
**Subcategory:** Object response

## What you see

Dropped coins or XP orbs first scatter and bounce, then once the player is close (or after a delay) they accelerate toward the player and get absorbed with a small flash and number.

## Not to be confused with

Not a pickup sparkle (VFX-16, a shine on an item lying on the ground); magnetism is the movement toward the player.

## Use it for

Removes the chore of walking over every item and makes collecting satisfying; used heavily in survivor-likes.

## Tunable parameters

scatter time · magnet radius · acceleration · max speed · absorb flash · stacking of "+N"

## Request prompt

In your game, coins dropped by an enemy bounce outward for 0.4 s; then if the hero is within 48 px they home in with 600 px/s² acceleration (max 220 px/s), and on contact play a small yellow flash and show "+1" above the hero.

## Implementation hint

Pure CPU motion on pickup entities (homing: v += normalize(player-p)*accel*dt; clamp). Render in the sprite batch; absorb flash uses the additive particle batch.
